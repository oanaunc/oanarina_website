Privacy Policy — Next Birthday Reminder

Last updated: August 29, 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Bundle ID: com.nextbirthday.app
Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com

Summary

Next Birthday Reminder (“the App”) keeps birthdays, reminders, notes and gift ideas together on your iPhone. Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. We do not collect your data, we do not show ads, and nothing you add is ever uploaded anywhere.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not operate servers that receive your personal data. We do not collect, store, or sell:

  • Name, email, or contact details (unless you email us for support)
  • The birthdays, names, photos, notes or gift ideas you keep in the App
  • Your Contacts, in whole or in part
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers
  • Payment information (the App is free)

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • The people you add: first and last name, day and month of birth, and the year if you choose to include one
  • Optional photos, relationship labels, favourites, and free-text notes
  • Gift ideas, whether each has been given, and your gift history by year
  • Reminder preferences, both the app-wide defaults and any per-person overrides
  • App settings such as theme, accent colour and display options

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to back up your iPhone through Apple’s iCloud or local backup services, in which case it is included in your device backup like any other on-device app data.

Contacts Access

Importing from Contacts is entirely optional. The App works without it, and you can add every birthday by hand instead.

If you grant permission, the App reads only what it needs to build the import list: first name, family name, the birthday saved on the contact, and the contact thumbnail. It shows you that list and imports nothing until you select people and tap Import. Contacts you do not select are never copied into the App.

Access is read-only. The App never modifies, adds to, or deletes anything in your Contacts, and it never transmits your Contacts anywhere. You can revoke permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Contacts; the birthdays you already imported stay in the App.

Photos

When you attach a photo to a person, the App uses Apple’s system photo picker. The picker runs outside the App, so the App receives only the single image you chose and never gains access to your photo library. The image is resized and stored locally alongside that person.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your device. Nothing is sent through a push server, and no notification content leaves your iPhone. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings, or change which reminders you receive in the App under Settings > Reminders.

Widgets

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a small copy of your upcoming birthdays — name, date, and a thumbnail — from a shared container on your device. This is a local file that only the App and its widget can read. Widget data is never transmitted.

Export and Import

Settings > Data lets you export your birthdays as a plain JSON file, and import one back. The file is created on your device and goes wherever you send it using the standard iOS share sheet. We never receive it, and we have no way to read it.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services. We do not share your data with anyone.

Quick actions such as Message, Call and WhatsApp simply open those apps with the conversation ready. The App does not send anything on your behalf, and what happens after you hand off is governed by that app’s own privacy policy.

Analytics and Tracking

We do not use analytics SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites. The App does not display advertisements.

Children’s Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your birthdays, notes, photos and gift ideas remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Deleting a person removes their notes, gift ideas and gift history along with them. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from your iPhone. Export first if you want to keep a copy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or support: oanarinaldi@gmail.com
