Privacy Policy — AI Architecture Plan to Video

Last updated: 1 October 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Bundle ID: com.oanarinaldi.aiarchitectureplantovideo
Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com

Summary

AI Architecture Plan to Video turns a picture you choose — a 2D floor plan, a sketch, or a photo of a plot, a room, a facade or a render — into an AI result picture (for example a 3D model or a redesign) and a short video. The only AI provider it uses is Google (Gemini). There is no login requirement, no advertising and no analytics. Your projects, pictures, result pictures and videos are stored on your device. You can use the app in two ways:

  • With your own Google (Gemini) API key — your picture goes directly from your device to Google. We receive nothing.
  • With a plan or credits bought in the app — your picture passes through our server, which asks Google to make the result picture and the video and holds the result until your device collects it (see below).

What Stays on Your Device

  • The pictures you choose and the AI result pictures, in the app’s private container.
  • Finished videos, which the app saves into a “Plan to Video” album in your Photos library (you can turn this off, or keep an extra copy in the app, in Settings). The app asks for Photos access so it can play its own videos from that album; “Limited” access is enough. It does not read or upload your other photos.
  • Your projects, favourites and video settings, and a list of videos still being made so they can be collected after the app is closed.
  • Your API key, if you add one, in the iOS Keychain (this device only, never backed up or synced).
  • Your preferences.

Using Your Own API Key

The app sends your picture, the result picture, your style choices and notes directly from your device to the Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), authenticated with your key. Generation runs on Google’s servers; Google keeps generated videos for about 2 days so the app can download them. This data is processed under your own account and the provider’s terms: Gemini API Additional Terms. Some Gemini models in preview may use submitted content to improve Google’s products; check Google’s terms for your account.

Using a Plan or Credits

The app uploads your picture, the result picture, your settings and notes to our server (hosted on Cloudflare). The server stores the uploads only while your result picture or video is being made and deletes them when it finishes. It asks Google to make the result picture (Gemini image) and the video (Gemini Omni Flash or Veo), using the developer’s Google account. The finished image or video is kept on our server so your device can collect it even if the app was closed or offline: it is deleted one day after your device has downloaded it, or after 30 days if it is never collected.

To manage your credits and jobs, the server stores:

  • a random account ID created by the app (not linked to your name, email or Apple ID, unless you choose Sign in with Apple);
  • your App Store purchases (product, dates and transaction IDs), as signed by Apple;
  • your credit balance and a history of changes to it;
  • for each result picture or video: date, settings, the instructions sent to the AI provider, status, credits used and an estimated processing cost (kept up to 90 days for support);
  • if you allow notifications, the push token used to tell your device that a video is ready.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. A server record is only created once you buy a plan or credits, or sign in with Apple.

Sign in with Apple (Optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive only Apple’s anonymous identifier for you in this app — never your name or email — and use it to make your credits available on all your devices. Apple also gives us a token that we use only to cancel the sign-in when you delete your account.

Consent

The app asks for your consent before your first picture is sent to Google. Without it, generation features do not work.

Photos, Camera, Files and Notifications

The app uses only the images or files you select. Please avoid photos that show people or personal information; if they do, they are sent to Google with the rest of the picture. Camera access is requested when you take a picture. Photos access is used to save your videos and play them back; the app only reads the videos it created. PDF import uses the first page. Notifications are optional and only used to tell you that a video is ready or could not be made.

Advertising and Analytics

The app has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service. It does not request location or contacts.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Deleting Your Data

In the app, open Settings > Plan & credits > Delete account & data. This immediately deletes your account record, credit balance and history, job history, any uploads or videos still on our server, and any Sign in with Apple link, and revokes the Sign in with Apple token. Remaining credits are lost. Apple transaction IDs are kept, no longer linked to any account, only so the same purchase cannot be credited twice. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in your Apple ID’s Subscriptions settings. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

To remove everything on your device, use Settings > Delete all projects & videos, or delete the app. Remove your API key in Settings or revoke it in your Google account. For data held by Google under your own key, contact Google directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
