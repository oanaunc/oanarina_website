Privacy Policy for Solar Match

Last updated: October 8, 2026

A private little universe

Solar Match 2.0 is a native iPhone and iPad puzzle game made by Oana Rinaldi. Endless play and today's daily challenge are free. Solar Plus is an optional Apple-managed subscription. The app requires no account, contains no advertising or tracking, and does not send gameplay data to a developer server.

What stays on your device

Your saved endless board, high scores, completed challenges, recent run history, achievements and preferences are stored in the app's local storage. Existing high scores from the original game are migrated locally. The developer cannot access this information. iOS device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; a device backup may retain a copy.

Optional subscriptions and Apple

Solar Plus Monthly and Solar Plus Yearly use Apple's StoreKit in-app purchase system. Apple processes payment and provides the app with signed transaction and subscription status information needed to verify access, restore purchases and handle renewals, expiry or revocation. We do not receive your payment card details, billing address or Apple Account password. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Restoring purchases may contact Apple. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled through your App Store account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage Apple subscriptions.

Challenges and generated artwork

Daily boards and weekly expedition missions are generated locally using the date and fixed game rules. No gameplay information is sent to an AI service. The app includes pre-generated cosmic background artwork; using these backgrounds does not upload your information.

No advertising or analytics

Version 2.0 does not include advertising, analytics, tracking identifiers, third-party crash reporting, or Unity services. Earlier versions were built with Unity; this policy describes version 2.0 and later.

Support

If you email us voluntarily, we receive the address and information you provide so we can answer your request. Please avoid sending payment details or sensitive personal information. For game issues, describe the mode, your iOS version and what happened. Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com.

Children and choices

Gameplay does not ask for personal information from children or adults. Apple controls purchase authorization and parental settings. You can continue using the free game without a subscription.

Terms and policy changes

Subscriptions provide access while active; free endless play remains available. Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA) apply. Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised date.
