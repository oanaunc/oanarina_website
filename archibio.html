Oana Rinaldi

Architecture Bio

Technical architectural life

Studies

2013 – 2016 - PHD developing the thesis: "Non-standard geometry in contemporary architecture" at "Ion Mincu" University of Architecture and Urbanism, Bucharest, Romania.
2006 – 2012 - "Ion Mincu" University of Architecture and Urbanism, Bucharest, Romania, Architecture Department (Bachelor & Master's degree).
2008 – 2009 - IUAV University of Architecture, Venice, Italy, Architecture Department - study scholarship.

Professional Experience

Practicing architecture from Romania, to Italy, The Netherlands and Israel, I have approached design in many angles, from creating small housing units to landscape design and public area proposal. In the last years my focus was on 3D Modelling of furniture.

Exhibitions

Exhibitions and publications of architecture

  1. Publication: „Designing with Algebraic Surfaces as the Basis of Cyberspace Architecture”, in EURAU 2016 European Symposium on Research in Architecture and Urban Design: In Between Scales, editor dr. arch. Beatrice-Gabriela Jöger, Bucharest, „Ion Mincu” Publishing House, 2016, pg. 65-70.

  2. Participation at the International Conference "Re[Search] through Architecture ICAR 2015", at UAUIM, Bucharest, Romania (26-29 March 2015).

  3. Publication: „The Use of Algebraic Surfaces in Architectural Design”, in Re[Search] through Architecture ICAR 2015. Abstracts, editor conf. dr. arch. Andra Panait, Bucharest, „Ion Mincu” Publishing House, 2015, pg. 167-169.

  4. Participation at the exhibition"Interiors in movement" presenting architectural projects made during the course "Interiors Architecture", for the academic year 2008-2009, at Cotonificio Veneziano, Dorsoduro 2196, Venice, Italy (March – April 2009).

  5. Participation with group projects in the book: Pittaluga, Franca, Architetture mutevoli, Roma, Aacne, 2009, pg. 55.
