Seek a Word — Android Terms of Use

Updated: November 2, 2026. Developer: Oana Rinaldi. Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com.

Using the app

Seek a Word is a single-player word search game. All original categories, unlimited classic puzzles, optional diagonal words, sounds, vibration, pause/resume, saved progress, undo/redo, best times, result sharing, and today's daily challenge are free. No ads or account are required.

Optional Seek Plus

Seek Plus adds twelve themed collections, a rolling 30-day daily archive, custom puzzles with a saved draft, printable puzzle and answer-key PDFs, Zen and Sprint modes, starting-letter hints, and a local progress journal with streaks and six achievement badges. Sprint provides a three-minute target and allows finishing after that target. Monthly and yearly plans unlock identical features. Google Play displays localized prices and billing periods before purchase. No free trial is promised.

Subscriptions, cancellation, and refunds

Payment is charged through Google Play at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before the current period ends. Manage or cancel through Google Play subscription settings. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Google Play's payment, refund, and subscription terms apply. Restore Purchases checks subscriptions on the same Google Play account. Apple purchases and Google Play purchases are separate and do not transfer between stores.

Your device and puzzle data

Your game, history, best times, custom puzzle drafts, and settings stay on your device. Automatic Android cloud backup and app-data transfer are disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local progress. Restore Purchases restores eligible paid access, not local history. Use only content you are entitled to include in a custom puzzle. Sharing opens your device's share chooser; you choose whether and where to share.
