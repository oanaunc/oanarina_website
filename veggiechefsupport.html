VEGGIE CHEF

How can we help?

Veggie Chef brings 100 vegetarian recipes, a shopping list and step-by-step Cooking Mode to iPhone, iPad and Android. The cookbook works offline and requires no account.

Cook and shop

Open Recipes to search by name or ingredient. Open a recipe to adjust portions, add ingredients to Shopping List, schedule a meal or start Cooking Mode.

Veggie Chef Pro

Use Create from Ingredients or Surprise Me to open the AI Chef. An internet connection and active Pro subscription are required. Open My Kitchen, choose Veggie Chef Pro, then tap Restore Purchases to restore a subscription using the Apple or Google account that purchased it.

To manage or cancel a plan, go to Apple Subscriptions or Google Play Subscriptions.
