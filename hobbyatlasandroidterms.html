A little clarity about Plus.

Hobby Atlas is free to download. Discovery, search, unlimited dreams, scheduling, reminders, journaling and backup are free. Plus unlocks seven-day starter paths, the discovery challenge, Passion DNA, annual recap and optional on-device coaching on supported hardware.

Current prices, currency and billing periods come directly from Google Play and are shown before purchase. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before renewal. Manage or cancel them in Google Play. Lifetime is one payment for the on-device Plus features. No hosted AI, cloud generation or external classes are included. Device, language, region and model requirements apply to optional AI.

Restore Purchases uses your Google Account. No Hobby Atlas account is required. Purchase verification requires Google Play connectivity. Refunds and cancellation follow Google Play policies. Apple and Google Play purchases are separate and do not transfer between stores. Personal memories can move between editions through compatible backup files.

Hobby ideas, materials and estimated time/cost bands are introductory suggestions. Costs vary by location and equipment. Seek appropriate instruction for unfamiliar tools, sports, food preparation and outdoor activities. This is not professional health or safety advice. AI can make mistakes; check practical suggestions before acting.
