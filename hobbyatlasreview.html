Hobby Atlas helps people discover hobby ideas, keep a dream list, plan a first experience, and reflect on what they enjoyed. It is a native iPhone and iPad app with no app login.

Open or download the walkthrough video. The feature guide below is also available as text.

About this recording. The walkthrough is recorded in Simulator. Any demonstrated purchases use Apple’s local StoreKit test environment; no real charge is made. This is an explanation of the app’s flows, rather than a demonstration of a live App Store transaction.

The recording does not demonstrate AI generation on a physical device. The optional AI coach requires iOS 26 or later on supported physical hardware with Apple Intelligence enabled, a ready model, and a supported language and region. Curated guides and all other Plus features work without AI.

What to look for in the walkthrough

  1. Explore a universe of hobby ideas.Pan and zoom through colorful hobby bubbles, browse twelve categories, use the list alternative, or search the 504-item catalog. Core discovery works offline.
  2. Find a small first step.Open a hobby to see its beginner experiment, suggested materials, approximate starting time and cost, and related interests. Discovery filters help match available time, budget, location type, and energy.
  3. Keep a dream.Save hobbies to an unlimited dream list, choose a journey state, group them in named collections, or draw an idea from the curiosity jar.
  4. Make a little plan.Create a one-time or weekly hobby session. Local notifications and Apple Calendar export are optional and ask permission only when chosen. Exported calendar events are independent copies.
  5. Keep a memory.Reflect with a note, enjoyment rating, and optional selected photo. Journal content, dreams, and plans remain on the device. Backups can be exported and restored in My World.
  6. Explore Plus.The crown on Explore opens the Plus screen, including purchase restoration. Plus unlocks flexible seven-day starter paths, a discovery challenge, Passion DNA from reflections, and an annual recap. On supported devices, an optional on-device coach can also brainstorm hobby ideas. Monthly and yearly subscriptions offer the same Plus features; lifetime is a one-time purchase of on-device Plus access.

Free access and privacy

The full catalog, search and filters, unlimited dreams, scheduling, local reminders, basic journal, and backup are free. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs, no developer-hosted AI service, and no account-based social network. Apple processes in-app purchases. The optional coach uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models; journal notes and photos are not supplied to it.

Reviewers can skip the optional interest selection at onboarding and use the app immediately. No demo account or password is needed.
