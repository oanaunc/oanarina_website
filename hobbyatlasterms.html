Hobby Atlas is free to download. Discovery, search, dream lists, scheduling, reminders, journaling, and backup are free. Plus unlocks seven-day starter paths, the discovery challenge, Passion DNA, and the annual recap.

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage and cancel them through your Apple Account. A lifetime purchase is a one-time non-consumable entitlement to the on-device Plus features. No hosted AI, unlimited cloud generation, or external classes are included.

Restore Purchases uses your Apple Account and does not require a Hobby Atlas account. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies. Subscriptions and lifetime access do not transfer to a future Android edition.
