Privacy policy

Your curiosity is private.

Hobby Atlas — Discover Your Next Passion
Effective November 22, 2026

Hobby Atlas does not require an account. We do not run advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and the app does not send your dreams, interests, schedule, journal, or photos to a developer server.

Your data is stored in the app’s protected storage on your device. Your device’s system backups may include it according to your Apple settings. There is no automatic in-app sync. Uninstalling the app may remove your memories; export a backup first.

When you choose a photo, Apple’s photo picker gives the app access to the item you select. We store a resized copy with your journal. We do not request full-library access.

Notifications are scheduled locally after your permission. Calendar access is requested only if you choose to export an event. Apple Calendar then stores that event under its own settings. Removing a plan in Hobby Atlas does not remove a previously exported calendar event.

Apple processes purchases and provides the app with verified purchase entitlements. We do not receive payment card details. Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

The optional Plus AI coach uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models only on supported devices with Apple Intelligence available. It processes the question you type, the selected hobby or suggested catalog ideas, and your selected interest categories locally. No journal text or photos are sent to the coach. Questions and answers are not persisted by the app; you may choose to share an answer through the system share sheet. Device, language, region, and model availability requirements apply.

Exports contain your personal notes and photos. You decide where to save or share them. Importing a backup replaces the app’s current local memories after confirmation. Purchase access is restored separately through Apple.

You can remove saved dreams, plans, and journal entries inside the app. Removing the app deletes its local storage according to iOS behavior; backups and exported copies are managed separately by you.
