Oana Rinaldi

Art Bio

Artistic life

Studies

2023 – 2026 - PHD developing the thesis: "Exploring the Philosophy of Art in the Age of Artificial Intelligence. An analysis of contemporary aesthetics" at "National Art University", Bucharest, Romania.
2012 – 2014 - National Art University, Bucharest, Romania; Creative Strategies in Painting Department, (Master's degree).
2009 – 2012 - National Art University, Bucharest, Romania; Painting Department, (Bachelor's degree).

I am a certified photographer since September 2022.

Professional Experience

Being an artist from a young age made me explore painting and drawing at a professional scale. From personal to group exhibitions, to creating art albums and teaching art to students, I have tried to take advantage of every possibility that art gave me to express my ideas and emotions to the public.
