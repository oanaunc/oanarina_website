Support — AI Architecture Plan to Video

Need help? Email oanarinaldi@gmail.com. If it’s about credits or a purchase, include your Support ID (Settings > Plan & credits > Copy).

How it works

  1. Upload a 2D floor plan (Photos, camera or Files; PDFs use the first page).
  2. Pick a style, the opening scene, the final shot, light, format (9:16 or 16:9), length and resolution.
  3. The app builds a 3D model image of your house on the same sheet. Check it against your plan; regenerate it or upload your own render.
  4. Make the video. It takes about 1–5 minutes and you can close the app meanwhile — you’ll get a notification when it’s ready.

Getting a Google API key (to use the app for free)

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Choose Create API key and select or create a Google Cloud project.
  3. Enable billing on that project — Google’s video models have no free tier.
  4. Copy the key and paste it in the app: Settings > Your own API keys > Google.

Typical cost on your Google account: about $0.35 for a 10-second 360p draft, about $1.00 for 10 seconds at 720p, and about $0.10 for a 3D model image. An OpenAI key is optional and only changes which model draws the 3D model. The Gemini API is not available in every country — use credits instead if your key is refused for your location.

Learn library

The Learn tab has 20 free illustrated guides — reading floor plans, styles and materials, filming architecture, and new building technologies such as mass timber, Passive House and 3D printing. It works offline and needs no key or credits.

Credits

  • 3D model image: 1 credit. Video: 2 credits (360p draft), 4 credits (720p HD) or 7 credits (1080p Full HD).
  • Monthly credits are used first and refresh on your renewal date; top-up credits never expire.
  • If a video fails, is blocked by a safety filter, or is never collected within 7 days, its credits are returned automatically.
  • Restore purchases: Settings > Plan & credits > Restore. Sign in with Apple keeps credits on all your devices.

My video hasn’t arrived

  • Open the app: unfinished videos are checked and downloaded automatically. Interrupted downloads resume.
  • Allow notifications (Settings > General) to be told when a video is ready.
  • With your own key, Google keeps a finished video for about 2 days — open the app within that time.

Tips for better videos

  • Use a straight-on, evenly lit plan of one floor, cropped to the drawing.
  • Readable room labels and a marked road side or gate help the model.
  • Spend time on the 3D model: the video can only be as faithful as the plan and the 3D model it starts from.
  • Try ideas as 360p drafts, then make the final video in HD or Full HD.

Videos are AI-generated presentation concepts, not construction documents.

Deleting your data

Settings > Plan & credits > Delete account & data. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
