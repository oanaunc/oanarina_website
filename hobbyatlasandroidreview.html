Google Play review · Android edition

A little tour of Hobby Atlas.

A real Android emulator walkthrough of Explore → Discover → Save → Plan → Reflect. The app includes the same 504 hobby ideas and starter content as the iPhone and iPad edition.

The video shows free features with synthetic demonstration notes. No purchase or AI generation is shown. Every core feature is available without an app account or payment.

Plus adds seven-day starter paths, discovery challenges, Passion DNA and annual recaps. Google Play reviewers receive a complimentary access code in the private App access instructions. The optional AI coach additionally requires a physical device with supported Gemini Nano services; curated Plus features work without AI.
