Using Photo Filter Studio

Photo Filter Studio provides local, non-destructive photo editing for Android. You may use the app to edit photos and export your work for personal or commercial purposes. You must have the rights or permission needed to use the photos, signatures, and other material you edit or share. Your photos and exported work remain yours.

Free editor and optional Studio tools

The original 115 looks, existing adjustments and effects, and full-resolution standard exports are available without a subscription. Studio adds 24 looks, recipe saving and sharing, batch editing, selective light, auto enhancement, frames, and signature watermarks. Both Studio plans provide the same features. You can preview Studio creative edits without subscribing; exporting those edits, saving or importing recipes, and batch editing require an active Studio subscription. Removing Studio edits keeps your free adjustments and crop.
