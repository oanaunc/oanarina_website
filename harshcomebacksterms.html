Harsh Comebacks — Android Terms

Updated 29 October 2026. Developer: Oana Rinaldi. Package: com.oanarinaldi.harshcomebacks.

Using the app

Harsh Comebacks provides clean humor and authored replies for entertainment and self-expression. Use the content kindly and lawfully. Do not use the app to harass, threaten, or discriminate against anyone. Reply Studio chooses prewritten lines on your device; it is not an AI service and does not upload your messages.

Free features and optional Pro

The original 10,000 comebacks, unlimited Next, favorites, text sharing, phone widgets, and Wear OS companion are free. Optional monthly and yearly auto-renewing Pro subscriptions unlock situation packs, three tones, Reply Studio, Card Studio, search, collections, practice, and Daily Five. Both plans provide the same access. The app shows Google Play’s localized price and billing period before checkout.

Subscriptions, cancellation, and refunds

Payment is charged through Google Play at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before the current period ends. Manage or cancel through Google Play subscription settings. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Google Play’s payment, refund, and subscription terms apply. Restore Purchases checks active subscriptions on the same Google Play account. Apple and Google Play purchases are separate and do not transfer between stores.

Your local content

Favorites and collections stay on your device. Expiration does not delete them; the original Saved list remains free. Exported Card Studio images leave the app only when you choose to share or save them. Uninstalling removes local app data. Temporary Reply Studio input and practice drafts are not uploaded.
