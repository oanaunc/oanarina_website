Sculptural purses, imagined as wearable art

Romantic silhouettes, gathered fabrics and luminous details turn each purse into a statement of individuality. Explore the textures and the complete look; select any image to open the full editorial gallery.

01

Blue Amour

Royal Blue Heart-Shaped Statement Bag

Blue Amour brings together romantic elegance and sculptural design in a custom-designed heart-shaped purse. Luminous royal-blue fabric gathers into asymmetric folds, with sparkling crystal-like embellishments tracing its contours and catching the light across its surface.

A slender silver-tone chain completes the interplay of soft draping and luminous detail. Styled with a matching blue dress against warm classical architecture, this small piece of wearable art celebrates individuality and the expressive power of fashion.
