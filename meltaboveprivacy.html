Melt Above
privacy policy.

Effective 14 October 2026. Developer: Oana Rinaldi.

1. The game

Melt Above is an offline native iPhone and iPad game. It does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. The game has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, or third-party SDKs.

2. Local progress

Stars, level records, unlocked cosmetic treatments, and preferences are stored on your device. We do not receive this information or run a game server that stores it.

3. Your choices

Use Settings → Reset garden progress to delete your records and cosmetic unlocks. Removing the app removes its local app data. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings and may retain app data according to those settings.

4. Support and this website

The support page opens only when you choose it. Website hosting providers may process ordinary technical request information needed to deliver web pages. These pages add no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or contact form.

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to include, and use it to investigate and respond to your request. Include only what is necessary, and do not send passwords, private documents, or other sensitive information. Contact us about support correspondence you would like corrected or deleted.

5. Children

The game does not request personal information from children and contains no advertising or purchases. It does not require camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo-library access.

6. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions, contact Oana Rinaldi at oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com.
