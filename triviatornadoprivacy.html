Privacy Policy for Trivia Tornado

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Applies to version 1.1 and later.

1. Your privacy

Trivia Tornado is an offline trivia game by Oana Rinaldi. No account is required. We do not collect or transmit your gameplay, personal quiz content, player names, scores, bookmarks, or preferences to our servers. We do not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party trivia APIs.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores scores, answer statistics, completed challenge dates, recent round history, saved and missed question identifiers, settings, and personal quiz questions locally. Quiz-night names are used for the current round. These records are not sent to us. Depending on your device settings, local app data may be included in an Apple device backup. You can erase local progress and personal quizzes in Settings; this does not cancel a subscription.

3. Optional subscriptions

Classic gameplay is free. Tornado Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription offered through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes payments and manages billing information under its own privacy policy. The app uses StoreKit to verify subscription access on your device. We do not receive your payment card details or operate a subscription-account server. You can restore purchases or manage and cancel your subscription from the app's Settings or your Apple Account.

4. External links and support

Opening a privacy, terms, subscription-management, or support link takes you to an external website or your email app. Those services may process information under their own policies. If you choose to email support, we receive the information you include and use it to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive information unnecessarily.

5. Children and permissions

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Apple controls purchases and parental purchase approvals.

6. Your choices and changes

You control local data and device backups through the app and your Apple settings. Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised date. Terms of Use: Apple Standard EULA.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or app support, email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com.
