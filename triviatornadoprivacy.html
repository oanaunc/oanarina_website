Privacy Policy for Trivia Tornado

Last Updated: 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to Trivia Tornado! This privacy policy is to inform users about our practices concerning data protection. Kindly read it thoroughly to understand how we handle your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Trivia Tornado does not collect, store, or share any personal or non-personal information from our users. The app is designed to be used without the need to provide any personal data.

3. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect any data, it is safe for use by children under the guidelines of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Rule (COPPA).

4. Your Rights and Choices

Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no user data rights or choices applicable within the app.

5. Security

While the app does not collect or store personal data, we ensure that any future update that might involve data collection will prioritize user data security and comply with applicable data protection laws.

6. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

Any changes to our privacy policy will be posted on this page. Users are encouraged to review our privacy policy periodically to stay informed about how we are helping to protect the personal information we collect.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@oanarinaldi.com
