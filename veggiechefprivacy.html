Veggie Chef Privacy Policy

Veggie Chef is a vegetarian cookbook and optional AI recipe service from Oana Rinaldi. The app works without a Veggie Chef account.

Your cookbook and kitchen

The offline cookbook is bundled with the app. Favorites, cooking history, personal recipes, grocery items, meal plans and the recipes you save stay in the app’s storage on your device. They may be included in device backups you enable. You can remove individual items in the app or remove the app to delete its local storage. Timers use optional notifications scheduled on your device.

AI Chef

Before your first AI request, Veggie Chef asks for permission to share your ingredients and recipe choices with OpenAI. If you allow it, the app sends these inputs to our recipe service hosted by Cloudflare, which sends them to OpenAI to generate a recipe. Avoid putting personal information in ingredient fields. Reset your AI data permission from the AI Chef screen at any time.

The recipe service does not store recipe prompts or responses in its own database. It requests that OpenAI disable response storage. OpenAI may retain API data temporarily for abuse monitoring as described in OpenAI’s API data controls. Cloudflare processes network information to deliver and secure requests.

Purchases and usage limits

Apple or Google Play processes subscriptions for your platform. When you use AI Chef, the app sends proof of purchase to our recipe service. The service checks Apple-signed transaction proofs or verifies Android purchase tokens with Google Play. It uses a subscription identifier to verify access and enforce usage limits. The service keeps a monthly count and recent request times per subscription. Your app store handles payment card details.

Reporting an Android AI recipe

If you submit a report in the Android app, Veggie Chef receives the selected generated recipe and your reason. We use this information to review the report and improve the service. To ask for removal of a report, contact us at the address below.

Tracking, questions and privacy requests

Veggie Chef uses no advertising trackers and does not sell personal information. To ask about your data, request deletion of server-side usage records or get help with privacy settings, email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com.
