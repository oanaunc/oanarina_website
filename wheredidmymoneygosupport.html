WHERE DID MY MONEY GO? ✦

App Support

Effective 22 October 2026

Track your money. Your way.

Need a hand with Where did my money go? Email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com. Please include your device, OS version, app version and a short description. No account or app password is needed.

Getting started

Choose a display name and currency, then create a money list from a template. Enter the allowance and repeat rule. Tap a list to record a purchase or add money. Explore Demo contains clearly labeled sample data; Start fresh switches to your own lists.

Understanding your allowance

A fresh-reset list starts each new period at the original allowance; carry-forward adds the next allowance to the remainder. Neither rule deletes history or represents a real bank deposit. Transfers between lists do not count as spending or income. Savings goals track progress separately and do not increase the dashboard total.

Backups and a new device

Use Settings → Export a backup to save a JSON copy. On another device, use Restore from backup. Keep your original backup until you verify the restore. The app has no automatic cross-device synchronization. CSV export is for spreadsheets and cannot be restored as an app backup.

Notifications, lock and widgets

Enable only the reminders you want. Permission can be changed in your device Settings. App lock requires an existing device passcode/screen lock. Widgets hide budget amounts while app lock is enabled. Add widgets through the iPhone/Android home-screen widget picker.

Plus and restore purchases

Plus adds planned payments, forecasts, annual analysis and printable PDF reports. Purchase or restore using your native app store account. Restore does not restore local money records; use your JSON backup for those. If product prices aren’t available, check your network/store connection and try again. Free tools do not require a purchase.

Canceling a subscription

On iPhone/iPad: Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app or starting fresh does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests go through the store.

Deleting data

Export anything you want to keep, then choose Settings → Start fresh. Deleting exported copies and system/cloud backups is separate. There is no app account to close. If you contacted support and want your support correspondence removed, email the address above.

Privacy

The app does not collect your financial journal, use advertising trackers, or require a login. See the privacy policy and terms of use.
