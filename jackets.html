Wearable Art · One-of-a-Kind

Handmade Couture Jackets

Embellished by hand, designed to be remembered.

Oana Rinaldi Fashion Atelier

Jackets transformed into singular works of wearable art

Each piece begins with a classic silhouette and becomes entirely its own through embroidery, hand-applied flowers, bows, pearls, sequins and sculptural details. Explore every jacket from the front, back and close up; select any image to open the full editorial gallery.

01

Crimson Poppy Nocturne

A vivid red biker jacket softened by hand-built poppies and deep botanical embroidery. The clean asymmetric front gives way to an opulent garden across the back—a contrast between structure and romantic abundance.

02

Emerald Secret Garden

Deep emerald suede becomes a quiet landscape for blush roses, mint bows and jewel-toned butterflies. Transparent mesh allows the floral composition to float across the jacket like a pressed garden preserved in motion.

03

Champagne Winged Heart

A warm champagne jacket with a restrained floral front and an unforgettable metallic heart across the back. A feathered butterfly rests on its edge, turning the piece into a tender emblem of movement, affection and transformation.

04

Fuchsia Blossom Rhapsody

Fuchsia suede meets pale blush embroidery, pearl chains, crystalline centers and sculptural flowers. Romantic without becoming delicate, this jacket balances vivid color with intricate couture detailing.

05

Azure Beaded Garland

A clear turquoise silhouette conceals an exuberant back: sequined flowers, pearl-centered blossoms and densely beaded leaves form a luminous garland. Tone-on-tone ornament makes the craftsmanship emerge gradually as the light moves.
