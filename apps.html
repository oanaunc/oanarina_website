Goldbach's Conjecture

Education

Free App

iOS

Discover one of the greatest unsolved problems in mathematics — the Goldbach Conjecture — in an interactive way! This app allows you to explore the conjecture that every even integer greater than 2 can be written as the sum of two prime numbers. Simply enter an even number of your choice, and the app will instantly calculate and display a pair of prime numbers whose sum equals your input. Whether you're a math enthusiast, a student, or just curious about prime numbers, this app makes it easy to experiment with numbers of any size — from small values to extremely large integers. Fast calculation of prime pairs, even for very large numbers. Elegant, easy-to-use interface designed for quick exploration. Educational and fun — great for students, researchers, and anyone curious about number theory!
