AI Meal Tracker

Food & Drink

Free App

iOS

AI Meal Tracker is a private food diary that turns meal photos into nutrition estimates — calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, and sodium — so you can hit your daily goals without spreadsheets or sign-ups. Snap or pick a photo and AI estimates what's on your plate, search foods or scan barcodes powered by Open Food Facts, edit any value, or log manually anytime. Your daily diary includes a day-by-day meal log with a calorie goal ring, macro breakdown, Quick Add for favorites and recent meals, and optional logging reminders. Track trends with today's macro donut chart, a water intake tracker with daily goal, weekly calorie trends and streaks, and a weight log with smooth charts (1W / 1M / 3M / All), optional target weight line, and optional Apple Health sync. Private by design: no account, no cloud, no sign-up — meals, water, and weight stay on your iPhone. Photo analysis uses your own OpenAI API key (stored in the iOS Keychain); food search, barcode scanning, and manual logging work without a key. Set calorie, protein, and water goals with a built-in calculator, choose kilograms or pounds, and see calories left and latest weight on a home screen widget. AI estimates are for general wellness tracking only — not medical advice.

4D Rubik's Cube Explorer

Games

Free App

Web App

4D Rubik's Cube Explorer is an interactive application that brings the classic Rubik's Cube into the fourth dimension. Instead of solving a standard 3×3×3 cube, users can explore and manipulate a 3×3×3×3 hypercube puzzle, where each visible structure represents a projection of a higher-dimensional object. The app is designed to make 4D geometry more intuitive through play. Users can rotate, inspect, and interact with the puzzle while learning how pieces, stickers, cells, and moves behave in a four-dimensional space. By translating abstract mathematical concepts into a hands-on visual experience, the application helps users understand spatial relationships that are normally difficult to imagine. Beyond being a puzzle, the app functions as an educational tool for exploring higher-dimensional thinking, computational geometry, and game-based learning. It invites players, students, researchers, and puzzle enthusiasts to ask a simple but fascinating question: what would a Rubik's Cube look like in the fourth dimension?

Fun Flags

Education

Free App

iOS

Take off and paint your way around the globe! Fun Flags turns world geography into a relaxing, hands-on adventure. Hop in your plane, fly across a beautiful world map, and land in a new country. Great for curious kids and grown-ups, geography fans, classrooms, and family game time. No pressure, no timers — just you, the world, and a box of colors. How many flags can you collect?

Goldbach's Conjecture

Education

Free App

iOS

Discover one of the greatest unsolved problems in mathematics — the Goldbach Conjecture — in an interactive way! This app allows you to explore the conjecture that every even integer greater than 2 can be written as the sum of two prime numbers. Simply enter an even number of your choice, and the app will instantly calculate and display a pair of prime numbers whose sum equals your input. Whether you're a math enthusiast, a student, or just curious about prime numbers, this app makes it easy to experiment with numbers of any size — from small values to extremely large integers. Fast calculation of prime pairs, even for very large numbers. Elegant, easy-to-use interface designed for quick exploration. Educational and fun — great for students, researchers, and anyone curious about number theory!
