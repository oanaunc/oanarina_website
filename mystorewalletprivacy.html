My Store Wallet

Privacy Policy — My Store Wallet

Last updated: June 26, 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com

Overview

My Store Wallet (“the App”) is a loyalty-card wallet for iPhone and Apple Watch. We designed the App to work without an account and without collecting your personal information.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not operate servers for the App. We do not collect, store, or sell:

  • Name, email, or contact details (unless you email us for support)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or advertising identifiers
  • Payment information
  • Your loyalty card numbers on our servers

Information Stored on Your Device

When you add a card, the App saves it locally on your iPhone in a file inside the App’s private storage. This includes:

  • Store or card name you enter
  • Barcode number and format
  • Color and optional note you choose

This data never leaves your device except when you sync cards to your paired Apple Watch via Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework (Bluetooth/Wi‑Fi between your own devices).

Camera

The App requests camera access only when you choose to scan a barcode. Camera images are processed on-device to read the barcode; we do not record, upload, or store photos or video.

Third Parties

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or social logins. Apple provides the operating system, App Store distribution, and device-to-watch sync.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. It is suitable for general audiences.

Data Retention and Deletion

Cards remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes all stored cards.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: oanarinaldi@gmail.com
