Sport Reps Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Sport Reps Tracker is a free workout planner and log for iPhone and iPad, developed by Oana Rinaldi. The app works offline and requires no account.

Data on your device

The app stores your routines, exercises, set results, notes, calendar plans, and settings in its local storage. Optional profile information and photos you choose for routine covers are also stored there. The app uses Apple’s photo picker to access the photo you select.

The app has no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. Your workout data stays in local app storage unless you use an export or Apple Health feature. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by Apple under your device settings.

Notifications

With your permission, iOS can deliver local workout reminders and rest timer alerts. You can change these choices in the app or in system settings.

Apple Health

If you choose Save to Apple Health and grant permission, the app writes the workout type and start and end times to Apple Health. The app requests permission to write workout records only. Set details, personal notes, and profile information stay in Sport Reps Tracker. Apple Health manages the workout record after export. You can manage permissions and delete exported records in Apple Health.

Export, retention and deletion

You can export a backup or workout CSV through Profile → Data & Privacy. The file is saved to the location you choose, which can include a cloud storage provider. You control who receives copies of that file.

Choose Erase all app data to remove your records and settings from the app on that device. The app’s working data is also removed when you delete it. You can separately remove saved backups and any workouts you exported to Apple Health.

Contact

If you email oanarinaldi@gmail.com for support, we use your email and the information you choose to share to respond to your request.
