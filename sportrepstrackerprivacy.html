Sport Reps Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Sport Reps Tracker is a free workout planner and log for iPhone, iPad, and Android, developed by Oana Rinaldi. The free workout log works offline and requires no app account. Optional Sport Reps Plus subscriptions add training tools.

Data on your device

The app stores your routines, exercises, set results, notes, calendar plans, and settings in its local storage. Optional profile information and photos you choose for routine covers are also stored there. The app uses your device’s photo picker to access the photo you select.

The app has no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. Your workout data stays on your device unless you choose to export it. The app has no service that receives your workout data. On iPhone and iPad, Apple manages device backups under your device settings. On Android, the app disables automatic backup and you can choose where to save a manual backup.

Sport Reps Plus and purchases

Apple or Google processes optional subscription payments through your store account. The app receives store purchase information to verify access, restore purchases, and confirm transactions. Payment card details are handled by the store and are not received by the app or developer. The developer may see transaction and subscription reports provided by the store.

Training plans, progression suggestions and muscle reports are calculated on your device. We do not send your workout history to an AI provider or developer server. On Android, Plus must connect to Google Play to check your subscription when the app starts; free workout logging remains available offline. Store providers handle purchase data under their own privacy policies.

A workout backup contains your workout records and settings, not a transferable subscription entitlement. Restore purchases using the store account that made the purchase. Apple and Google Play subscriptions are managed separately.

Notifications

With your permission, your device can deliver local workout reminders and rest timer alerts. You can change these choices in the app or in system settings.

Apple Health

If you choose Save to Apple Health and grant permission, the app writes the workout type and start and end times to Apple Health. The app requests permission to write workout records only. Set details, personal notes, and profile information stay in Sport Reps Tracker. Apple Health manages the workout record after export. You can manage permissions and delete exported records in Apple Health.

Health Connect on Android

If you choose Save to Health Connect and grant permission, the app writes the exercise session type and the workout’s start and end times to Health Connect. The app requests permission to write exercise sessions only. It does not read Health Connect data or use it for ads, analytics, or profiling. Set details, personal notes, and profile information stay in Sport Reps Tracker.

You can revoke permission and delete exported sessions in Health Connect under your device settings. Sport Reps Tracker’s use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Export, retention and deletion

You can export a backup or workout CSV through Profile → Data & Privacy. The file is saved to the location you choose, which can include a cloud storage provider. You control who receives copies of that file.

Choose Erase all app data to remove your records and settings from the app on that device. The app’s working data is also removed when you delete it. You can separately remove saved backups and any workouts you exported to Apple Health or Health Connect.
