Using the app

Sudoku Hero is a single-player puzzle game. Classic games at all five difficulty levels, notes, educational hints and technique guides, undo/redo, mistake tracking, pause/resume, automatic saving, best times, and result sharing are free. No account is required. New puzzles have a single solution; difficulty labels describe clue-count targets and do not guarantee a specific solving technique.

Optional Hero Plus
