Privacy Policy — Where U At

Last updated: 1 September 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com

Overview

Where U At (“the App”) lets you share your location with specific people you invite and who accept, and see theirs in return. It is for iPhone and iPad. It works without an account, without a password and without any server of ours.

This app does handle location data — that is the whole point of it — so this policy explains exactly what is collected, where it goes, who can see it, and how to stop it.

What the App Collects

When you grant permission, the App reads your device’s location from Apple’s Core Location service. Alongside each position it records the time the reading was taken, your device’s battery level and your device’s name.

It also stores the display name, photo and pin colour you choose for yourself.

The App does not collect your email, contacts, payment details, advertising identifiers or any usage analytics. It contains no analytics SDK, no advertising, no social logins and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Where Your Location Goes

Your location is written into the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit. From there it is shared with the specific people you have connected with, using Apple’s CloudKit sharing.

Each person you connect with gets their own separate, private storage area that only they can read. That is how the App can let you pause sharing with one person while continuing to share with everyone else — they are not all reading the same copy.

Sharing is mutual and always begins with an explicit invitation. Somebody must send you a link or a QR code, and you must accept it, before any location passes between you. Nobody can add themselves to your map.

People you share with can see: your position on a map, when it was last updated, your battery level, and the name, photo and pin colour you chose.

What We Never See

We do not operate servers for the App. Your location is never sent to us and we have no way to look at it — no dashboard, no export, no access of any kind. The storage belongs to your iCloud account, which Apple provides and we cannot read.

We could not produce your location history if we were asked for it, because we never receive it.

No Location History Is Kept

The App stores only your most recent position. Each new reading overwrites the last one. There is no trail of where you have been, on your device or in iCloud, and neither you nor the people you share with can look back at where you were yesterday.

This is a deliberate design decision. A history that does not exist cannot be leaked, subpoenaed or misused.

Background Location

If you grant “Always” permission, the App updates your position while it is closed, so the people you share with see where you are now rather than where you were the last time you opened it. If you grant only “While Using”, or nothing at all, the App still works — your position simply stops updating when you close it.

Background location is used solely to keep your shared position current. It is never used for advertising or profiling.

Saved Places Stay on Your Device

If you save places such as Home, Work or School to get an alert when somebody arrives or leaves, those places are stored in your own private iCloud database and are never shared with anyone — not with us, and not with the people you are connected to.

Arrival and departure alerts are worked out on your own device. The person you are watching is not told that an alert exists, and neither are we.

Notifications

The App registers for background notifications from Apple’s iCloud service. These are used for one purpose only: to tell your device that somebody’s position changed, so the map can update. They are never used for advertising and we never send you messages.

Your Controls

  • Pause for everyone — for an hour, until tonight, until tomorrow, or until you switch it back on.
  • Pause for one person — without disappearing from anyone else. While paused, no new position of yours is written anywhere that person can read.
  • See who can see you — the Privacy tab lists every person who can currently see your location.
  • Remove a connection — this deletes the copy of your location that person was able to read.
  • Delete all app data — removes every connection and every saved place.
  • Revoke location entirely — in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services at any time.

Third Parties

The App includes no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or social logins. Apple provides the operating system, iCloud storage, maps and App Store distribution.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. In parts of the EU and EEA the minimum age for consenting to this kind of processing is higher — between 13 and 16 depending on the country. If you are below the age that applies where you live, please do not use the App without a parent or guardian’s involvement.

Because location sharing is a serious matter for younger users, a child should only use the App with a parent or guardian’s knowledge, and a parent or guardian should review who the child is connected to.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your current position and your connections remain until you remove them in the App or uninstall it. Removing a connection deletes the copy of your location that person could read. Uninstalling removes everything stored on that device.

To remove the iCloud copy as well, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on your iPhone, select Where U At, and delete its data. This control is provided by Apple and works whether or not the App is still installed.

Legal Basis for Processing (UK, EU and EEA)

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, the data controller is Oana Rinaldi, contactable at oanarinaldi@gmail.com.

We rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)) to process your location and share it with the people you accept. The App asks for that consent on its own screen, before it reads any location, and it will not process anything until you give it. You can withdraw it at any time in the App under You › Withdraw Agreement, which is as easy as giving it was and which also deletes the data.

Location data is processed only to provide the App’s core function: showing your position to people you have explicitly accepted. It is never used for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making or any form of tracking.

Providing your location is not a legal or contractual requirement. If you decline, the App still installs and opens — you simply will not appear on anybody’s map.

Your Rights

If you are in the UK, EU or EEA you have the rights below. Because your data lives in your own iCloud account and never reaches us, most of them are exercised directly in the App or in iOS rather than by writing to us — which is faster, and means you never have to prove who you are to anyone.

  • Access — everything the App holds about you is visible in the App itself: your profile, your connections, your saved places and your current position.
  • Rectification — edit your name, photo and pin colour at any time under You › Profile.
  • Erasure — use Delete All App Data or Withdraw Agreement in the App, and delete the iCloud copy under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
  • Restriction and objection — pause sharing for everyone or for one person at a time, at any moment, from the map or the Privacy tab.
  • Withdrawing consent — under You › Withdraw Agreement. This stops all processing and deletes the copies other people could read.
  • Portability — the App keeps only your most recent position and a short profile; there is no history to export. If you would like a copy of what exists, write to us and we will explain how to retrieve it from your own iCloud account, since we cannot retrieve it for you.

We cannot access, correct, export or delete your data on your behalf — we have no copy of it and no route to it. If you believe we have handled your data improperly, please contact us at oanarinaldi@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

International Transfers

Storage, and any transfer of data between countries, is carried out by Apple as part of iCloud and governed by Apple’s own privacy terms. We neither choose nor control where iCloud stores your data, because none of it passes through us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: oanarinaldi@gmail.com
