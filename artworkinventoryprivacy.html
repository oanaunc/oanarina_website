Privacy Policy

Artwork Inventory by Oana Rinaldi
Bundle identifier: studio.artworkinventory.app
Updated November 1, 2026

Your private artist archive

Artwork Inventory stores artwork records, original images, attachments, notes, exhibition and project links, contacts, locations and financial details locally in the app’s protected storage. No account is required. We do not operate a public gallery, sell your archive, or include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics or third-party crash reporting SDKs.

Photos, camera and files

Apple’s photo and file pickers provide only the items you choose. Camera permission is requested when you choose to take a photograph. You can also create a record without a photograph and attach documents, audio or video. Imported files are copied into your local archive; deleting a record does not delete the original in Photos or Files.

Optional AI, exclusively for Pro

AI suggestions require an active paid Pro subscription and your confirmation before sending. Only a resized JPEG of the artwork you select is sent over HTTPS to our service and OpenAI. Private notes, contacts, financial records and location history are not included in that request. The JPEG is newly encoded for the request; the original file is not uploaded. Review every suggestion before applying it.

Our service uses OpenAI’s API with storage disabled for the response. We do not intentionally retain uploaded images or generated descriptions on our server. OpenAI may retain API data for abuse monitoring according to its API data policies; API data is not used to train its models by default. Network providers necessarily process connection information such as an IP address to deliver and protect the service. Do not submit an image containing sensitive information you do not want processed.

Purchase verification and usage

Apple processes optional monthly and annual Pro subscriptions. We do not receive your payment card details. StoreKit verifies purchases on your device. For AI requests, the app sends Apple’s signed transaction to our server, which verifies the subscription with Apple. To enforce the 100-request allowance and protect against abuse, the service stores a one-way hash of the original transaction identifier, allowance-period dates, request status and usage counts. This usage information is linked to a purchase, not to the contents of your archive. Signed transactions and request bodies are not intentionally written to application logs. See Apple’s App Store privacy information.

Widgets, locking and backups

Artwork images, titles and years you choose for the widget are copied into a shared container used only by the app and its widget. These can be visible on your Home Screen even when the archive lock is enabled. Device authentication is handled by iOS; we do not receive Face ID biometric data or your passcode. Device backups follow your Apple settings. The app does not provide an automatic cloud-sync account.

Archive folder backups, CSVs, PDFs and other exports contain the information you choose to export. A full archive backup includes private records and media. Protect these files and the destinations where you save or share them. Other apps and services handle shared files under their own policies.

Deletion and your choices

You can edit or delete records within the app. Unused cached media may be cleaned up separately from the active database. Removing the app deletes its local archive; external copies, Photos originals, shared exports and device backups must be managed separately. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Contact us to request deletion of server-side usage data; we may need limited purchase information to locate it, and may retain information necessary to prevent abuse or meet legal obligations. Do not email an entire private archive.

Support and policy changes

If you contact support, we receive what you choose to send and use it to address your request. This professional cataloguing app is not specifically directed at children. Policy changes are published here with a revised date.
