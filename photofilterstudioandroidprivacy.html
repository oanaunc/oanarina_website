Privacy Policy

Photo Filter Studio by Oana Rinaldi
Android package: com.oanarinaldi.photofilterstudio
Updated October 23, 2026

Your photos stay on your device

All filters, adjustments, selective lighting, frames, signatures, and batch editing run locally on your device. The developer does not upload or receive your photos, image metadata, edits, recipes, signatures, or photo library. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

Photos, camera, and files

Android's photo and document pickers provide only the photos and files you select. The app does not request broad access to your photo library or storage. Camera opens your device's camera app to take a photo you choose. Edited copies are saved through Android's media library or document picker; originals are not overwritten.

Local storage and deletion

Recent editing sessions store an original working copy, a thumbnail, and reversible editing settings in private app storage. The current working edit can be recovered after the app closes. Saved recipes contain editing settings, a name, an identifier, and a creation date. Preferences and favorite filter identifiers also remain on the device. App backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Use Settings to clear recent edits or reset favorites, and the recipe menu to delete recipes. Uninstalling the app removes its private working files and preferences. Exported images remain in Photos, Files, or the destination you selected until you delete them separately.

Metadata, signatures, and sharing

Exports can retain source metadata, including location information, when Preserve Metadata is enabled. Choose Remove Location to exclude GPS metadata, or turn Preserve Metadata off to omit source metadata. Batch exports omit source metadata. A signature entered into the editor is drawn into the resulting photo and is visible to recipients. Android's system sharing interface sends only the image or recipe you choose to the destination you select; that destination's privacy policy applies. Copy Image places an edited image link on your device clipboard.

Optional Studio subscriptions

Monthly and annual Studio subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google processes purchase, payment, renewal, cancellation, and refund information under Google's Privacy Policy. The app queries localized product prices and current purchases, checks Google's purchase signatures, and acknowledges completed purchases through the Google Play Billing Library. Purchase data is used to provide Studio access. The developer has no payment server, does not receive payment-card details, and does not send photos to Google Play Billing. Accessing products, making purchases, and restoring purchases require a connection to Google Play. The free editor works offline.

Both plans unlock the same Studio tools. The original 115 looks, existing adjustments and effects, and standard full-resolution exports remain free. Studio edits may be previewed for free and removed before exporting with free tools. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Support and policy changes

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not send payment-card details. This general-purpose photo editor is not specifically directed at children. A parent or guardian should supervise purchases. Updates to this policy are published on this page with a revised date.
