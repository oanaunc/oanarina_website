Privacy Policy — Draw Through

Last updated: October 23, 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Apple bundle ID / Android package: com.oanarinaldi.drawthrough
Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com

Summary

Draw Through (“the App”) is an offline drawing course, reference library, practice journal, and composition app for iPhone, iPad, and Android. The App works without an account or sign-in. Your drawings, reflections, course progress, compositions, and imported reference images stay on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not operate servers for the App. We do not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details, unless you email us for support
  • Your drawings, reflections, lesson progress, bookmarks, photos, reference images, compositions, project names, or favourites
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or tracking data
  • Payment information

Photos and Reference Images

When you add a reference image, the App uses the system photo or document picker provided by iOS, iPadOS, or Android. You choose which image to provide to the App. Draw Through uses that image only as a lesson reference or composition layer and to save your work locally. Android does not request access to your entire photo library. Your images are not uploaded to us or to a third-party server.

Saving to Photos

When you choose Save to Photos, the App asks iOS for permission to add the exported composition to your photo library. On Android, the App adds the requested exported image through Android’s media storage API without requesting broad photo-library or storage access. The App writes an image only after you request the export. You can manage applicable permissions in your device Settings. Exported images remain in your photo library until you remove them there.

Data Stored on Your Device

Lesson drawings, written reflections, study completion and progress, bookmarks, project names, favourites, layer settings, transforms, opacity and appearance adjustments, thumbnails, and copies of selected reference images are stored locally in the App’s private storage so your work can be saved automatically and reopened later.

Sharing

If you use the system share sheet, you decide which app, person, or service receives the exported image. The App does not send it anywhere by itself. Any service you choose through the share sheet processes the shared image under its own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

Draw Through does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not display advertisements, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not share your data with third parties.

Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your studies and projects remain on your device until you clear or delete them in the App, clear the App’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App. Clearing a lesson drawing preserves its written reflection. Saved studies can be revisited in My practice. Deleting a composition removes its locally stored project data and thumbnail. On Android, imported image copies that are still used by another composition are retained for that composition. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data, subject to any device backups you control through Apple. The Android version excludes its private app data from cloud backup and device transfer. Images you explicitly export or share are separate copies and are not removed when you uninstall the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or support: oanarinaldi@gmail.com
