Privacy Policy — Draw Through

Last updated: August 30, 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Bundle ID: com.oanarinaldi.drawthrough
Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com

Summary

Draw Through (“the App”) is a drawing reference and composition app for iPhone and iPad. The App works without an account or sign-in. Your compositions and imported reference images stay on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not operate servers for the App. We do not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details, unless you email us for support
  • Your photos, reference images, compositions, project names, or favourites
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or tracking data
  • Payment information

Photos and Reference Images

When you add a reference image, the App uses Apple’s system photo picker. You choose which image to provide to the App. Draw Through uses that image only to display it in your composition and to save your project locally. Your images are not uploaded to us or to a third-party server.

Saving to Photos

When you choose Save to Photos, the App asks iOS for permission to add the exported composition to your photo library. The App writes the image only after you request the export. You can manage Photos permission at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Data Stored on Your Device

Project names, favourites, layer settings, transforms, opacity and appearance adjustments, thumbnails, and copies of selected reference images are stored locally in the App’s private storage so your work can be saved automatically and reopened later.

Sharing

If you use the system share sheet, you decide which app, person, or service receives the exported image. The App does not send it anywhere by itself. Any service you choose through the share sheet processes the shared image under its own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

Draw Through does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not display advertisements, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not share your data with third parties.

Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your projects remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Deleting a composition removes its locally stored project data, thumbnails, and reference-image copies. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data, subject to any device backups you control through Apple.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or support: oanarinaldi@gmail.com
