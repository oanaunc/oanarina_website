Photo Editor

Oanarina Photo Editor

Your images. Your imagination.

A native Mac workspace for photo editing, layered compositions, painting and typography. Bring photographs, textures, type and ideas together, with your files kept on your Mac and no account required.

Get the app Explore the tools

Free · macOS 26.5 or later

The Mac app workspace: your canvas, tools, layers and history in one place.

From the first layer to the final detail

A space to create

Build with layers

Combine images with groups, blend modes, opacity, clipping masks and painted layer masks. Keep each piece of your composition editable.

Find your color

Refine light and color with curves, levels, exposure, hue and saturation. Explore white balance, color balance, gradient maps and fourteen editable adjustment types.

Make precise selections

Work with rectangle and ellipse marquees, lasso and magic wand. Expand, contract or feather selections to refine an edge.

Paint & retouch

Paint with adjustable brushes, erase, clone and heal details. Soften with blur, shape with smudge, and blend with gradients.

Compose with type

Add editable text, adjust its font and spacing, and combine it with shapes. Align and distribute layers, rotate the canvas, or correct a photographed surface with four-corner perspective crop.

Keep your creative flow

Switch between projects in tabs, use familiar keyboard shortcuts and click any retained History state to go backward or forward. Save layers or export the finished image.

Start anywhere. Make it yours.

From an everyday photo to a layered poster, give an idea room to take shape.

1. Bring something in

Import a JPEG, PNG, HEIC or TIFF, or start with a landscape, square, portrait, story or custom canvas.

2. Explore the possibilities

Arrange layers, refine selections, retouch details and experiment with adjustments. Use History to follow your edits and undo or redo changes.

3. Save what you made

Save an editable .comp project, or export a PNG, JPEG or lossless TIFF to share your finished image.

A few useful details

Before you begin

Does it work offline?

Yes. Editing and project files stay on your Mac. The app does not require an account or upload your photographs to an editing service.

Can I keep editing my layers later?

Yes. Save your work as a .comp project to preserve its layers and editable composition. PNG, JPEG and TIFF exports are flattened images.

Which workflows are supported?

This version focuses on raster photo editing and layered compositions in sRGB. It does not currently include PSD import or export, RAW development, CMYK print workflows or third-party plugins.

Who makes Oanarina Photo Editor?

Oanarina Photo Editor is developed by Oana Rinaldi. Its native editing foundation is the MIT-licensed Compositor project by Wonder Assembly LLC. The original open-source license is included with the app.

How can I share feedback?

Report a reproducible problem or suggest an improvement through the contact page. Include your macOS version and the steps that led to the problem.
