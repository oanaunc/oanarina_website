Privacy Policy — Ai Shoes Design

Last updated: 12 October 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Bundle ID: com.oanarinaldi.aishoesdesign
Contact: oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com

Summary

Ai Shoes Design helps you imagine painted sneaker designs, outline views, stencil concepts and poems. Projects and the illustrated learning library are kept on your iPhone or iPad. AI processing is optional and requires your consent. You can use your own OpenAI API key, or buy an optional plan or credit pack to use our key securely through Cloudflare. We do not use advertising, analytics, third-party tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

What Stays on Your Device

  • Your imported shoe photos, extra angle photos, generated images, project names, creative instructions, saved poems and favorite flags.
  • Lesson reading progress and app preferences, including AI consent.
  • Your OpenAI API key, stored in the iOS Keychain with After First Unlock, This Device Only protection. It is not sent to our server or synchronized to another device.

The local project database and image directory are excluded from device backups. Export important designs before deleting the app. Local projects can be deleted individually in Projects or together in Settings. Keychain items can survive app removal; use Settings → Your OpenAI key → Remove stored API key to explicitly remove your key.

What Is Sent for AI Processing

When you request a design, outline, stencil or poem, selected shoe images and the relevant creative instructions are sent to OpenAI at api.openai.com. Photos are downsampled and re-encoded before upload, which removes embedded photo metadata. Extra angles may be combined into a labeled reference sheet. A poem request includes the selected design image, project title, style, palette and brief.

With your own key, the device sends the request directly to OpenAI under your account and API billing. With plans or credits, requests pass through our Cloudflare Worker and are forwarded to OpenAI using the developer’s API account. Your own key is sent only to OpenAI. We do not store input shoe photos or creative instructions in our application database. OpenAI may retain processing data according to its applicable service policies; see OpenAI’s privacy policy and API data controls. OpenAI and Cloudflare may process data outside your country.

AI consent is requested before first processing and can be turned off in Settings. Browsing the studio artwork, reading lessons and viewing saved projects does not send those images for AI processing. Validating an API key sends the key to OpenAI to check access.

Plans, Credits and Server Data

The app uses a random account ID, not a login or your name. This ID is kept in iCloud Keychain when available so credit access can persist across compatible devices. The app checks the balance using this ID; a server account record is created when purchases or credits need to be recorded. It stores the account ID, balance, signed Apple purchase identifiers, product and renewal information, credit changes and generation status, quality, output size and usage cost. Included poem requests record the account ID and request time to enforce the daily limit. We do not receive your payment card details.

Completed premium images are temporarily stored in Cloudflare KV for up to 24 hours so interrupted downloads can be recovered. Input photos, free-text briefs and generated poems are not stored in our database. Service providers may keep operational and security logs under their own policies. Undelivered paid image results are refunded automatically after the recovery window described in the app.

Optional plans use Apple In-App Purchase: Monthly gives 30 credits per month; Pro gives 100 credits per month. One-time packs give 15 or 50 credits. Actual localized prices are shown by the App Store before purchase. A standard image costs 1 credit and high detail costs 3; alternatives are charged per image. Monthly credits reset at renewal without rollover; pack credits do not expire. Poem requests are included for accounts with a plan or available credits, up to 10 requests per 24 hours. Own-key processing is billed by OpenAI.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account or the app’s Settings. Refund requests for purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting the credits account does not cancel a subscription.

Photos, Camera and Sharing

The system photo picker gives the app only images you select; it does not grant broad library access. Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photograph. Add-only permission is requested when you explicitly save a result to Photos. Sharing or exporting uses the iOS share sheet; content is sent to the destination you choose. Shared files may be retained by that destination.

Deletion and Your Rights

Settings → Delete local projects removes the local project database records and associated images and poems. Settings → Delete credits account removes the server account, unused balance, credit and generation history, poem request records and temporarily held results associated with that account. Remaining credits are lost. Apple transaction IDs are retained without an account link only to prevent a purchase from being credited twice. Deleting a local project does not itself remove temporary recovery copies on the server; these expire within 24 hours.

For access, correction or deletion requests, email the developer and include the Support ID shown in Settings. For data held under your own OpenAI account, contact OpenAI. You may have additional data rights under the laws applicable to you.

Children and Appropriate Use

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Upload your own shoe photographs or images you have permission to use, and avoid personal documents, faces and private details.

AI Concept Limits

AI instructions aim to preserve your shoe’s silhouette and construction, but results can contain mistakes. Handwritten text may require correction. Outline views and stencils are raster concepts, not verified measurements, CAD, manufacturing patterns or scale-accurate cutting templates. Hidden views may be invented. Check the physical shoe and material instructions before painting.

Changes and Contact

Changes will be posted here with an updated date. For help, visit Ai Shoes Design Support or email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com.
