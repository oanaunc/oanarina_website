Simple tools for your own money journal.

The app tracks amounts you enter. It does not hold funds, verify a bank balance, send money, pay bills, provide loans or offer financial, investment or tax advice.

Using the app

You are responsible for the accuracy of your entries and for keeping backups you need. Recurring lists represent budget allowances; a reset is a calculation and does not mean a real deposit occurred. Transfers move tracked allowance between lists only. Savings goals are independent progress trackers. Forecasts are arithmetic based on your saved rules and planned payments and cannot account for unrecorded spending or income.

Free features

Core lists, transactions, recurring allowances, history, calendar, goals, transfers, themes, reminders, widgets and JSON/CSV exports are available without an app account or subscription. Optional device permissions can be declined.
