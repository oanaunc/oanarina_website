Next Birthday Reminder

Next Birthday Reminder — Support

Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Bundle ID: com.nextbirthday.app
Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com

Getting Started

On first launch you can either import the birthdays already saved in your Contacts, or add someone by hand. You can do both later at any time.

Importing shows you every contact that has a birthday saved, preselects the ones that are new, and marks anyone already in the app. Nothing is imported until you tap the Import button.

Adding a Birthday

Tap + on the Upcoming or People screen. Give a first name, pick the month and day, and optionally add a photo, a last name, a relationship and notes.

You do not need to know the year. Turn off “Include year” and the birthday is saved as a day and month — the countdown still works, the age simply isn’t shown.

Reminders

Settings > Default reminders lets you pick as many offsets as you like — on the day, 1 day before, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month — and one time of day for all of them.

Any person can override the defaults: open them, tap Edit, and turn on “Use custom reminders”. Useful for keeping two weeks’ notice on family and just one day on colleagues.

Not getting reminders? Check iOS Settings > Notifications > Next Birthday and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Reminders are rescheduled every time you open the app, so opening it once will restore anything that was missed.

Widgets

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap +, search for Next Birthday, and pick a size.

  • Up Next — small, medium and large lists of who is coming up
  • Countdown — one person, one big number of days
  • Lock Screen — inline text, a circular countdown, or a compact card

Widget showing the wrong day or stuck? Widgets refresh at midnight and whenever you change something in the app. Open the app once to force a refresh. If it still looks wrong, remove the widget and add it again.

Gift Ideas

Open a person and add ideas as they come to you during the year. Tick one off when you have bought it and it moves into that person’s gift history, so next year you can see what you gave last time.

Common Questions

Does it work without an internet connection?
Yes. The app has no server and never goes online.

Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for.

Will my birthdays sync to my other devices?
Not yet. Everything is stored on the one iPhone. Use Settings > Data > Export to save a JSON copy, and Import to bring it into another device.

What happens on 29 February?
In non-leap years the birthday is shown on 28 February, so the person never disappears from your list.

Can I import from Facebook?
No. Facebook stopped giving apps access to friends’ birthdays years ago. Contacts import and JSON import are the supported ways in.

Someone was imported twice.
The importer flags likely duplicates by name and date, but you can always swipe left on a row in People and delete one.

Will it change my Contacts?
Never. Contacts access is read-only and optional.

Privacy

No account, no servers, no analytics, no ads. Everything you add stays on your iPhone.
