Privacy

A private space for your architectural ideas.

Effective 19 October 2026. AI Architecture Presentation is published by Oana Rinaldi. This policy explains the iPhone and iPad app’s data practices.

Your device is your library

Imported sources, generated studies, project notes, lesson notes, bookmarks and progress are stored locally. Project images are excluded from device backups. There is no cloud project sync. Export work you want to keep before deleting the app or changing devices. A random private account ID is stored in Apple Keychain to associate purchases and included credits; it is not an email login.

AI is a choice

Before the first AI request, the app asks for consent. Only the source image and brief you select are sent for that request. With your own OpenAI key, requests go directly from your device to OpenAI. Your key remains in Keychain and is not sent to our service. OpenAI bills your API account directly.

With included credits, the image and brief pass through our Cloudflare service to OpenAI. Our API key stays on the server. Our service does not save your source image, prompt or generated image. We retain a request reference, time, credit amount and completion status to prevent duplicate billing and return credits for failed generations.

OpenAI processes the content under its API policies, including applicable abuse-monitoring retention. Review OpenAI’s privacy policy and API data controls. Cloudflare processes the requests and stores the credit ledger; see Cloudflare’s policy. These providers may process data internationally. Do not upload confidential drawings, personal information or content you lack permission to use.

You can withdraw consent in You → Your data & AI consent. This prevents future generation until you consent again; it does not retract requests already processed by OpenAI.

Purchases and credits

Apple handles payment information. We do not receive card or bank details. Our service verifies Apple-signed transaction data and retains transaction references, product, purchase and expiry dates, refund status and the private account ID for billing, restoration and fraud prevention. Monthly credits reset on the verified subscription period and do not roll over. Purchased credit packs do not expire and do not unlock subscription features.

Reports and support

If you report a result, your description and optional request reference are saved for up to 90 days. The image and your key are not attached. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send. Never send an API key, payment details or confidential project documents.

No advertising or tracking

The app contains no third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. We do not sell your information or use it for cross-app tracking. Cloudflare and OpenAI may maintain operational and security records according to their policies.

Delete your data

Delete individual folios in the app, or use You → Your data & AI consent → Delete app data to remove the local library, key, learning records and server credit account. Unused credits are forfeited. Minimal transaction references and application/refund status remain without the account link to prevent replay and meet legitimate billing obligations. Deleted records can remain in provider backups for their normal recovery window.

Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel it in Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To request access, correction or deletion assistance, email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com. You may have additional privacy rights under applicable law.

Appropriate use

The app teaches architectural communication and produces conceptual images. It is not intended for children under 13. AI output is not measured CAD, structural engineering or evidence of building-code compliance. Check all imagery against project documentation and qualified advice.
