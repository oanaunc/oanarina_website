App license and your content

Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement governs your license to the app. You retain ownership of your artwork, photographs and records. Import and share only content you have permission to use. You are responsible for the accuracy of your records and for keeping independent backups.

Free archive and Pro subscription

The free archive includes unlimited artwork records and the existing manual details, exhibitions, projects, contacts, career tools, widget, backups, restore, CSV export, PDF portfolios, artwork sheets and authenticity certificate templates. Pro adds professional gallery and business documents, studio cost recording and proceeds insights, archive completeness checks and up to 100 AI requests per subscription month. Monthly and annual plans provide the same features. Allowance periods are calendar months anchored to the original purchase date; unused AI requests do not roll over. AI requires a verified active paid subscription, your confirmation and an internet connection. No free AI trial is provided.

Billing, renewal and cancellation

The App Store shows your localized price and billing period before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account may be charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of that period. Manage or cancel in Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple processes payments and refund requests. If Pro expires, the free archive and its existing exports remain available. Saved expense records remain readable, editable and exportable. Creating new studio costs, professional documents, archive checks and AI requests requires an active Pro subscription. Backup and CSV access remain available.

AI and certificate limitations

AI output is a draft based on visible image characteristics and can be inaccurate. Review it before applying or sharing it. It cannot verify authenticity, authorship, exact materials, creation dates, dimensions, provenance, ownership or monetary value. Certificate exports are unsigned templates for an artist to verify and sign; they are not independent authentication. The app does not provide professional valuation, insurance or legal advice.

Professional documents and financial records

New document tools create gallery labels, price lists, packing lists, consignment schedules, condition reports, invoice drafts and insurance schedules from the information you enter. They do not authenticate artwork, assess insurance value, process payment, file taxes, or replace agreements reviewed by the relevant parties. Verify every document and any applicable tax treatment before using it. Studio costs and recorded sales are displayed separately by currency, without conversion. Existing basic portfolios, artwork sheets and authenticity certificate templates remain free.

Service availability and privacy

AI depends on Apple purchase verification and external processing and may be temporarily unavailable. Monthly and daily service safeguards protect against abuse. Failed provider requests are not counted as completed AI suggestions. Local manual archive tools remain usable offline. Device storage and the operating system affect capacity, image processing and widget refresh timing. Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy.
