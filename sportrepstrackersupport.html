Support

For help with the app, email oanarinaldi@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS, iPadOS, or Android version, and what happened.

How do I log my first workout?

Tap Start Workout on Home to use the suggested routine or choose Open workout to add your own exercises. Adjust the values and tap Complete Set. Finish Workout saves your completed sets to Progress and Calendar.

How do I create a routine?

Open Workouts and tap the plus button. Name the workout, add exercises, set your targets, and arrange the exercises in the order you want. Open a saved workout to schedule it.

How do I resume a workout?

Tap Resume Workout on Home or tap the active workout above the tab bar. Your session is saved as you go, including if you close the app.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open Profile → Data & Privacy and choose Export JSON backup. On another device, open the same screen and choose Restore from backup. Restoring replaces that device’s existing data. Use Export workout CSV to open completed set entries in a spreadsheet.

How do I save a workout to my device’s health app?

Open a completed workout and tap Save to Apple Health on iPhone or Save to Health Connect on a supported Android device. Grant permission to write exercise sessions when prompted.

What stays free?

All original features remain free: unlimited workout logging, routines and custom exercises, full history and existing charts, scheduling, reminders, JSON/CSV exports and optional health exports. No app account or ads are required.

What does Sport Reps Plus add?

Version 1.1 adds optional Plus tools: four-week plans based on your equipment, schedule and time budget; next-session targets from two matching completed sessions; 28-day comparisons and sets by primary muscle; and warm-up/plate calculators. Open Sport Reps Plus from Home, Progress or Profile. Plans and workout data saved with Plus remain available after cancellation.

Can I try Plus before subscribing?

In version 1.1, open Profile → Sport Reps Plus → Try the Training Lab demo. Explore all four tools with temporary sample workouts, without an account or payment. Demo plans and targets stay separate from your real workout log and disappear when you close the demo. Plus applies these tools to your own training history and calendar.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

Open Sport Reps Plus and tap Restore purchases with the Apple or Google Play account used for the original purchase. Tap Manage subscription to change or cancel your plan through that store. Deleting the app or erasing workout data does not cancel a subscription. Apple and Google Play purchases are managed separately and do not transfer through a workout backup.

Both monthly and yearly plans include the same tools. The US base prices are $3.99/month and $29.99/year; the store shows your localized price before purchase. No payment is required to keep using the original free features.

Why are there no next-session suggestions yet?

Log two distinct matching sessions within 45 days for a weighted exercise. Both must have every set completed, the same set count and load, and matching reps between 8 and 12. Incomplete, skipped, assisted and varying-load sets do not generate suggestions. You can always edit your targets manually for free.

Why will a plan not save?

The four-week builder avoids dates with an existing workout or rest-day plan. Choose a different start date or move the conflicting calendar plans. Review the full preview before saving.
