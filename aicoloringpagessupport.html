Oana Rinaldi / Ink Studio

A little help for your creative practice.

Turn a photo or an idea into printable black-outline artwork, then color, save and share it.

Getting started

Choose one of eight Create modes. Photo to Page accepts a photo, camera image or image file; the other modes start with editable text. Select detail, page shape and line preferences. Confirm the upload before generating. AI results may vary, so inspect the outline before printing. Projects also includes three free practice sheets, and Learn contains 20 complete lessons.

Plans and credits

Standard generation costs 1 credit; High costs 3. Monthly plans provide 30 or 100 credits each renewal. Monthly credits reset and do not roll over. Purchased top-up credits do not expire and are spent after monthly credits. A new generation costs credits even when its idea is similar to an earlier one. Failed or uncollected generations are refunded automatically; uncollected or interrupted requests are reconciled when you next refresh your balance after two hours.

Own API key

Add your OpenAI API key in Settings and select Your OpenAI key as the generation method. The app does not charge credits for this route; OpenAI bills API usage separately. A ChatGPT subscription does not include API credits. The key must have image-generation access and billing enabled.

Purchases and account

Use Restore purchases in Settings while signed in to the Apple account used for purchase. Consumable credits depend on the app's account identifier in iCloud Keychain, so keep Keychain enabled for continuity. Manage or cancel subscriptions with Settings → Manage subscriptions, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For refunds, visit Apple's refund service.

Export and coloring

Open a project to export its PNG or an A4/US Letter PDF. The PDF contains the raster artwork fitted within printing margins. Use Color this page to draw with touch or Apple Pencil, undo strokes or erase your own coloring. Your original black-outline image is preserved.

Deletion and contact

Settings → Delete credit account removes server data and remaining credits but does not cancel subscriptions or delete local projects. For help, copy your Support ID from Settings and email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com. Never include an API key or payment details.
