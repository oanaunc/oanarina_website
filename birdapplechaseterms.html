Bird Apple Chase — Android Terms of Use

Updated: November 4, 2026. Developer: Oana Rinaldi. Contact: oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com.

Playing the game

Classic is a free, single-player apple-catching game. Hold left or right to steer your bird, release to stop, and stay off the ground. No ads, account, tracking, or developer server are required. Scores, settings, and journal records stay on your device.

Optional Chase Club

Chase Club adds Apple Sprint, Storm Survival, Zen Orchard, Daily Harvest, 30 Orchard Trail stages and a weekly trail, eight animated painted birds, six forest themes, goals, achievements, and a harvest journal with JSON export. Monthly and yearly plans include the same features. Google Play displays localized prices and billing periods before you purchase. There is no free trial.

Subscriptions and store billing

Payment is charged through Google Play at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the current period ends. Manage or cancel through Google Play subscription settings. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Google Play’s payment, subscription, and refund terms apply. Restore Purchases checks for eligible access on the same Google Play account. App Store and Google Play subscriptions are separate and do not transfer between stores.

Local records and availability

Your journal remains on this device after paid access expires. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local records. Android automatic cloud backup and app-data transfer are disabled. Export your journal using the system share chooser if you want a copy. Restoring purchases restores eligible paid access, rather than your local history. Classic remains playable offline. Paid access requires periodic Google Play checks, with a limited offline window after a successful verification. Daily courses reset at UTC midnight; weekly courses use UTC ISO weeks.
