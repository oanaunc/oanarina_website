Furniture

I use furniture as a canvas, working with the grain, edges and carved details of each piece to see how color can change its place in a room.

Chromatic Convergence

Two pieces of furniture form a single abstract composition. Diagonals carry the eye from midnight blue and turquoise across a field of yellow into magenta and red. Visible brushstrokes give the geometry a painterly edge, and the composition changes as you walk around it.
Hand-painted furniture · Mixed media on wood · 2026

The Art of Living in Turquoise

A single shade of turquoise brings together the open shelves, carved doors and ornamental trim of a vintage bookcase. Light catches the raised floral motifs, bringing the original carving into focus.
Painted vintage bookcase
