Privacy Policy for Fun Flags

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fun Flags! This privacy policy is to inform users about our practices concerning data protection. Kindly read it thoroughly to understand how we handle your personal information.

2. Learning Records and Optional Sharing

Fun Flags does not operate a developer account system or a server for your learning records. Game progress, points, streaks, collected flags, continent filters, preferences, quiz answers, review schedules, saved countries, custom quiz selections, daily scores and statistics are stored locally on your device. We do not receive individual study answers or use them for developer analytics or advertising. If you choose to share a result or progress summary through the system share sheet, it sends the content you select to the destination you choose.

Optional Google Play Games on Android

Android versions that include Google Play Games Services can authenticate an existing gaming profile through Google's platform. Google processes gaming-profile information and service analytics and diagnostics under its own privacy policies. Google's games services may also process device identifiers, IP addresses and country- or region-level information to operate, analyze and troubleshoot the service. Fun Flags does not request precise device location. Creating a new profile is optional; classic play and local learning work without a profile.

When you choose Connect & share records, Fun Flags sends the identifiers of earned achievements and your best country-quiz, capital-quiz and Sprint scores to Google Play Games for your gaming profile. This can include records already earned on the device. Your profile and scores may be visible to other players according to your Google Play Games privacy settings. Individual study answers, saved countries and the full learning journal stay on your device.

Pause sharing stops future achievement and score uploads from Fun Flags. It does not switch off Google's platform authentication or service diagnostics, delete records already shared with Google, or sign out your OS-managed gaming account. You can manage your profile, visibility and stored game data in Google Play Games settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data.

3. Offline Play and Store Purchases

Fun Flags has no advertisements or developer account requirement. Coloring, quizzes, flashcards and generated daily challenges work offline. Optional Explorer Plus subscriptions are purchased through Apple on iOS or Google Play on Android. Internet access may be needed for prices, purchases, subscription restoration and verification, and optional Google Play Games features. Apple or Google processes payments under its own privacy policy; we do not receive payment card details. StoreKit on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android provides purchase and subscription status to the app to determine access. Android verifies Google-signed purchase records using the app's public license key. We do not send these transactions to a developer server. Monthly and yearly subscriptions unlock the same Plus features; all classic functionality remains free. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings or Google Play subscriptions. iOS Terms of Use (Apple Standard EULA). Google Play Terms of Service.

4. Children's Privacy

Fun Flags is designed for curious kids and grown-ups, classrooms and family game time. There is no developer registration, advertising or developer analytics. Apple and Google manage purchase authorization on their respective platforms, including parental purchase controls where applicable. Google manages eligibility and parental approval for supervised Play Games profiles. Players can continue with local learning without connecting Play Games. Parents and guardians can manage a child's Google profile and permissions through Google's family controls.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Your learning records and preferences remain on your device. You can change saved countries and custom quiz selections in the app. Removing the app clears its local records; deleting local data does not cancel a store subscription or delete achievements and scores held by Google. Restore Purchases checks eligible subscriptions for your Apple or Google Account, but does not restore local learning records from a different device. Play Games stores achievements and leaderboard records for your profile; this integration does not back up or restore the full learning journal. Contact us with any privacy questions.

Delete Fun Flags Play Games data

First choose Pause sharing on the Google Play Games screen in Fun Flags to prevent local records from being uploaded again. Open your Google Play Games profile, choose Your Data, then Delete Play Games Account & Data. Under Delete individual game data, select Fun Flags and follow Google's deletion steps. You can also reach these controls through Play Store > Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings > Delete profile & game data. Google's deletion guide explains deleting individual game records or the entire Google-managed gaming profile.

This removes the selected Play Games data, such as achievements and leaderboard records, according to Google's deletion controls. Google manages its platform account, service-data retention and any separate Play History settings. Fun Flags has no developer-hosted account or learning-data server to delete. To remove local study records, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Deleting game data does not cancel Explorer Plus; manage subscriptions separately in Google Play or your Apple Account. For help, contact oanarinaldi@gmail.com.

6. Security

The app serves its bundled game content locally. Connections used by Google Play Games encrypt data in transit using HTTPS. Purchase processing and platform account controls are provided by Apple or Google. Local study records remain subject to the security of your device.

7. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

Any changes to our privacy policy will be posted on this page. Users are encouraged to review our privacy policy periodically to stay informed about how we are helping to protect the personal information we collect.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: oanarinaldi@gmail.com
