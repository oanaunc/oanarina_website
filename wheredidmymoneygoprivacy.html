WHERE DID MY MONEY GO? ✦

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Your money. Your device. No app login.

Where Did My Money Go? is a manual allowance and expense journal. We do not operate a user-account service, collect your financial records, sell data, show advertisements or use advertising/analytics trackers.

Information you enter

Your chosen display name, currency, money lists, purchases, top-ups, notes, transfers, savings goals, contributions, planned payments and preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to the developer. No email address, phone number or bank details are required to use the app.

Local storage and device security

On iPhone and iPad, records are stored using SwiftData in the app’s protected local storage. They may be included in your device backups according to your Apple settings; the app does not use CloudKit syncing. On Android, records are encrypted using Android Keystore and excluded from Android automatic backup and device transfer. Device loss, uninstalling the app or clearing app data may remove records. Save a backup if you want a portable copy.

Optional app lock and widgets

App lock uses the operating system’s biometric or device-credential authentication. The app never receives your fingerprint, face template or passcode. Widgets can show budget balances on your home screen; enabling app lock hides those widget balances. Consider who can view your unlocked device.

Notifications

If you opt in, reminders are scheduled locally by your device. They contain generic messages, not list names or money amounts. Permission can be changed in device Settings. The app does not use a remote push-notification service.

Subscriptions and stores

Optional Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. The app queries product prices and purchase status and verifies purchases using the relevant store tools. Your payment details remain with the store; the app and developer do not receive card details. Store-provided transaction information is used to unlock Plus. Apple and Google process store activity under their own privacy policies. Developer store dashboards may provide aggregate sales reports and platform-provided diagnostics.

Exports, imports and sharing

Backup files, CSV exports and PDF reports are created only when you request them. Exported files contain the records you chose to export and are not encrypted by the app. The system file picker/share sheet lets you choose where to store or send them. If you choose a cloud drive, email or another app, that destination processes the file under its own policies. Imports read only the backup file you choose; the app does not gain access to all your files. Temporary export copies are stored in the app’s private cache and older temporary copies are cleaned when exporting again.

External links and support

Privacy, terms and support links open this website outside the money journal. The website host may process ordinary web-request data such as IP addresses. If you email support, we receive the address and content you send and use them to respond and resolve your request. Do not send private financial records unless needed for a support issue; you can redact them.

Teens and families

The app is suitable for a general audience, including teens, and is not a banking service or a dedicated children’s account product. It does not collect children’s profiles, provide public communication, or include advertising. Purchases are controlled by your platform’s payment and family settings.

Retention and deletion

Local records stay until you edit, restore, reset or remove them, clear app data, or uninstall. Settings → Start fresh removes the current app records. Archived lists retain their history until a reset. Exported files and device/cloud backups are managed separately by you. There is no developer-held app account to delete. A store subscription is separate from local records and must be canceled through Apple or Google.

Your choices and contact

You can use the free app without purchases or optional permissions. You control backups, exports, notifications and lock settings. For privacy questions or a support-email deletion request, contact oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com. Policy updates will appear here with an updated effective date.
