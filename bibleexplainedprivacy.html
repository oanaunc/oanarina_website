Privacy policy

Updated 18 October 2026 · Developer: Oana Rinaldi

The Bible Explained provides offline Bible reading, historical and biblical prayers, original reflections and optional paid study tools. No account, advertising, analytics, tracking or location permission is required.

What stays on your device

Saved passages, highlights, notes, prayers, reading preferences, calendar entries and reminder schedules are kept in the app's local storage. We do not receive your journal. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your own backup settings. Exported journal files go only where you choose to share them. Deleting local data in Settings removes the app's saved journal and reminders; copies you exported or device backups must be managed separately.

Optional AI explanations

Before using AI, the app asks for explicit consent. When you choose Explain or submit a question, your selected passage, nearby scripture for context, reference, Bible edition, question, selected explanation perspective and answer language are transmitted over HTTPS through Cloudflare to OpenAI. A question may reveal religious beliefs or other sensitive information you choose to include. Your private saved prayers and notes are never automatically attached. Do not include personal information you do not want processed.

The service uses OpenAI's API with response storage disabled. We do not save prompts or answers in our database or intentionally log request bodies. OpenAI and Cloudflare process requests under their own service and retention policies; this is not a promise of zero retention by those providers. Requests necessarily expose technical network information such as IP addresses to the providers. Processing may occur outside your country, including in the United States. See OpenAI privacy, OpenAI API data controls, and Cloudflare privacy. Turn off AI consent in Settings to stop future AI submissions. Reading and searching do not require AI consent.

Purchases and service protection

Apple handles payments and subscriptions. We do not receive your card details. To authorize paid AI requests, our server verifies Apple-signed purchase details, including app and product identifiers, transaction identifiers, subscription validity and environment. Daily usage counters use a hash of the original purchase identifier; they are retained for up to 35 days. Refund and revocation records are kept until one day after the related subscription period expires. These records are used only to deliver the paid service and prevent abuse. They are not used for advertising or tracking.

Notifications and accessibility

Prayer reminders and timer alerts are scheduled locally after you grant permission. Notification wording does not contain your prayer or note text. Disable notifications in the app or iOS Settings. Read-aloud uses Apple's speech synthesizer and installed voices; it does not send your private journal to our AI service.

Your choices and rights

You can read without signing in, decline AI, change languages, export your journal, delete local data and cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Deleting the app or local data does not cancel an Apple subscription. For requests about service records, contact us; we may need a transaction reference to locate records without collecting more identity information than necessary. We do not sell personal information.

Children and content

The app is not directed specifically at children. Scripture contains mature historical themes; AI interpretation may make mistakes and is not a substitute for qualified pastoral, medical or other professional advice.
