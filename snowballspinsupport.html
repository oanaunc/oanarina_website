SNOWBALL SPIN

A little help for your next flight.

Tap to rise, glide through the ice, and fly a little further. Snowball Spin is available for iPhone and iPad.

Classic is always free

Unlimited Classic flights, the original glacier world, your best score, pause and restart, and milestone badges remain free. There are no ads or accounts.

Snowball Plus

Optional monthly and yearly plans unlock daily expeditions with three missions and a rolling 30-day archive, Zen flight, Blizzard rush, six worlds, eight snowball styles, four trails and a personal flight journal. Both plans include the same features. See the app for localized prices.

Daily missions

New daily routes arrive at 00:00 UTC. Each date has a repeatable route and three missions, which may be completed across separate attempts. Use Daily to revisit the last 30 routes. Zen continues after a bump; choose Pause → End flight to save your run.

Restore or cancel

To restore, open Settings → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that purchased the plan. To manage or cancel, open Apple subscription settings. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Classic stays free after Plus expires.

Your data

Scores, preferences and game progress stay on your device. There is no developer server, tracking, advertising or analytics. Apple handles purchase processing. Your previous Classic high score is preserved when updating. The new version requires iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.
