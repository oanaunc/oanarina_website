Your creative work

Your project library, selected source images, finished pages, coloring strokes, lesson progress and preferences are stored on your device. Device backups may include the app's local files. There is no advertising, tracking SDK or analytics SDK.

Before you generate

Each generation asks you to confirm sending your selected image and instructions for AI processing. Only use images you have permission to share. Free practice pages, lessons and local coloring do not require uploading an image.

Your own OpenAI key

Your API key is stored in this device's iOS Keychain. Requests go directly to api.openai.com; our server does not receive your key, input or result. OpenAI processes this information under its policies and your account terms. OpenAI API charges are separate from this app's subscriptions.

Plans and credits

When using app credits, your image and prompt pass through our Cloudflare service to OpenAI. Our server does not store source images or prompt text. Generated images are cached in Cloudflare for up to 24 hours so interrupted downloads can recover, then expire automatically. OpenAI may retain API information under its own policies. See OpenAI's API data controls.

Our service stores a random account identifier, your credit balance, Apple purchase identifiers and product/renewal details, credit adjustments, and generation records (date, quality, dimensions, credit cost, completion and provider usage). These records support purchases, generation recovery, refunds and support. They are linked to the random account, not your name or email. The account identifier is stored in iCloud Keychain to support continuity on your Apple devices. Apple handles payments; we do not receive payment card details.

Our hosting provider may process network information such as IP addresses for request delivery and security. We do not use it for advertising or tracking. Support emails contain the information you choose to send us.

Photos and camera

The system photo picker shares only the image you select. Camera access is requested when you take a photo. Add-only Photos access is requested only when you choose to save a finished page to Photos.

Retention and deletion

In Settings, choose Delete credit account to remove the server account, balance, generation history and cached results. Remaining credits are lost. Apple transaction IDs are retained without an account link to prevent duplicate crediting. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; use Manage subscriptions or Apple's Settings to cancel it. Delete individual projects from the Projects tab, or delete the app to remove its local library. Exported copies in Photos, Files or backups must be deleted separately. For information held by OpenAI under your personal key, contact OpenAI.

Credit and transaction records are kept until you delete your account, subject to the fraud-prevention record described above. Contact us with your Support ID for help with access or deletion. Never send your OpenAI key.

Children and changes

This general creative tool is not specifically directed at children. A parent or guardian should supervise image sharing and purchases. We will publish changes at this address with an updated date.
