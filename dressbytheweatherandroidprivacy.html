Dress by the Weather — Android Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026 · Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Android package: com.oanarinaldi.dressbytheweather

No account, advertising or tracking

Dress by the Weather does not require an app account and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Preferences, comfort feedback, saved cities, looks and packing lists are stored on your device. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. The developer does not operate a server that collects your weather requests, photos or purchase receipts.

Optional device location and chosen destinations

If you grant location permission, the app requests approximate location while it is open to obtain a local forecast and a place name. It does not request precise Android location permission or background location. You can decline permission and search for a city or address, or enter latitude and longitude instead. A searched destination can have precise coordinates even when device location permission is off. Coordinates are truncated to four decimal places before weather requests. Change permission in Android Settings at any time.

Weather requests and service logs

The app sends the requested coordinates directly over HTTPS to MET Norway Locationforecast. MET Norway also receives your IP address, which can reveal approximate location. Its API access logs may retain IP addresses and requested coordinates in its data centre in Oslo, Norway. This applies to current location and manually chosen destinations. Forecast responses are cached locally to reduce network traffic. These third-party requests are necessary to display live weather; the clearly labelled sample edition works without a live forecast.

MET Norway privacy policy · Weather service terms

City and address search

Search and place-name lookup use Android's geocoder, whose device-dependent provider may receive your search terms, coordinates for reverse lookup, and IP address under its own privacy terms. If no geocoder results are available, city and address search uses Photon's public service operated by komoot, with OpenStreetMap data. Photon receives the submitted search text and your IP address. Device location is not attached to manually typed city searches. Searches are debounced and limited to reasonable usage. Time zones are looked up offline from bundled data. Search and weather services may process information outside your country; their own policies govern their logs and retention.

komoot privacy policy · Google privacy policy · OpenStreetMap attribution

Reminders and widgets

Optional daily reminders are scheduled locally after you grant notification permission. Widgets read a locally stored snapshot of the last forecast opened in the app, including city, outfit tip and update time. Neither widgets nor reminders request location or download weather in the background. Open the app for fresh weather.

Pro wardrobe, photos and planning

Wardrobe pieces, optional garment photos, comfort ranges, laundry status, weekly plans, saved editorial inspiration and travel capsules remain in private app storage on your device. The system photo picker grants access only to photos you select. The app saves a resized image for wardrobe display, strips the original image metadata when re-encoding it, and does not upload photos or send them to an AI service.

Optional permanent Pro purchase

Dress Pro is an optional one-time purchase with no subscription. Google Play processes payment and handles its purchase records under Google's terms. The app uses Google Play Billing to query, verify, acknowledge and restore your Pro purchase, including entitlement changes after a refund. A signed purchase proof is kept privately on the device for offline verification and refreshed when Google Play is available. The developer does not receive payment card details or run a purchase-verification server. Purchases belong to the Google Account used in Google Play; Apple and Google purchases are separate.

Google privacy policy · Google Play terms

Your choices and deletion

Location, photo selection, reminders and Pro are optional. Delete individual looks, trips, capsules, wardrobe pieces and their photos in the app. “Delete saved Pro Studio data” removes wardrobe photos, weekly plans, capsules and saved editorial inspiration without changing your Google Play purchase. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall to remove all local preferences, caches, photos and signed purchase proof. There is no developer account database to delete. Clearing app data does not delete Google's purchase records or third-party API logs; contact the relevant provider about information it retains.

Contact

For support or privacy requests, email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com or use the app support page. Please do not include private addresses or location history in public issues. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request.
