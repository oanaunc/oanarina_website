Architecture 3D Walkthrough turns an image of one floor into an editable model you can explore on iPhone and iPad.

Build your first walkthrough

  1. Open the sample apartment to try the walkthrough offline, or import a floor-plan image from Files or Photos. Files also accepts a PDF with the plan on its first page.
  2. For an imported image, enter your pilot access code under Settings and choose OpenAI or Google Gemini. Tap Read floor plan and confirm the upload. You can browse other projects while the service works.
  3. Open Review and build. Check the openings and room boundaries against the source. If you know a room dimension, use Calibrate scale. Choose a ceiling height, an interior style and a starting room, then tap Build.
  4. Tap Open walkthrough. Switch between Walk, Dollhouse and Start tour. In Walk mode, use the arrow controls and drag to look around. The plan in the corner shows your position.

Create a rendered property tour

  1. Open Property tour in a reviewed project. Under Inside, tap Render interior tour to get images of the principal rooms, or preview and render a room individually.
  2. Under Outside, select a photo of the building or render an exterior concept based on the draft model. Choose the façade, street, elevated or dusk view.
  3. Tap Explore rendered tour to see the views on iPhone or iPad. Choose Animate this view in a render’s menu to create a short camera move, or Make a film to export your stills as an MP4.
  4. Use Publish shareable link to let clients explore the tour in a browser. You can update and unpublish the link from the same screen.

Rendered views are AI visualizations. Check the architecture, especially openings and exterior details, before sharing. Use Walk mode to move through the editable model.

Open it on a computer

Use Share HTML to save or send the file. On the computer, download the attachment and open it in a browser with WebGL 2 support. Use WASD or the arrow keys to walk and drag to look. The file includes its own viewer and opens offline.

Plan not recognized or room not reached

Try a clear straight-on image cropped to a single level, with dimensions and doors visible. Check that the two rooms meet at the door and that the opening fits on its wall. Ceiling height, materials and furnishings can be set during review. The walkthrough is a schematic visualization.

For help, email oanarinaldi@gmail.com. Include your device model and app version, and describe the step that failed.
