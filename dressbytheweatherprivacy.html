Dress by the Weather Privacy Policy

Privacy policy · November 26, 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi

No account, no tracking

Dress by the Weather has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Preferences, comfort feedback, saved looks, cities and packing lists are stored on your device. iOS device backups may include this local data.

Optional location

If you allow location access, the app requests approximate accuracy by default and uses your location while it is open. If you enable Precise Location in iOS Settings, the forecast request can use that more precise position. It uses this position to request a forecast. You can instead select any city or address. Change permission in iOS Settings at any time. Coordinates are truncated to four decimal places before forecast requests.

Weather requests and third parties

Forecast requests go directly to MET Norway. MET Norway receives the requested coordinates and your IP address. Its API access logs may retain these values in its data centre in Oslo, Norway. This applies to searched destinations as well as current location. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores these requests. Forecast responses are cached locally to reduce network traffic.

MET Norway privacy policy · Weather service terms

Place searches

City and address searches and place-name lookup use Apple MapKit. Search queries are sent to Apple under Apple's privacy terms. Location permission is not required for manually searching places.

Apple Maps & Privacy

Reminders and widgets

Optional reminders are scheduled locally after you grant notification permission. Widgets share a small local snapshot of the last forecast opened in the app, including the city and outfit tip. They do not request your location or poll the weather in the background.

Pro wardrobe and planning

Pro wardrobe pieces, optional photos, comfort ranges, weekly outfit plans, saved editorial inspiration and travel capsules are stored locally in the app. The system photo picker shares only images you select. The app saves a resized copy for wardrobe display; it does not upload your photos or send them to an AI service. You can delete wardrobe pieces and their photos in the app. iOS backups may include this local data.

Optional Pro purchase

Dress Pro is an optional one-time purchase with no subscription. Apple processes payment through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify, restore and maintain your Pro entitlement, including changes after a refund. The developer does not receive your payment card details and does not operate a purchase-verification server. Apple handles its purchase records under its own privacy terms.

Apple privacy policy · Apple standard app terms

Deletion

Delete saved looks and trips in the app. Removing the app removes its local data; copies may remain in iOS backups. You can manage those backups in iOS Settings. The developer has no account database to delete.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the app support page or the website contact section. Please do not include private addresses or location history in public issues. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request.
