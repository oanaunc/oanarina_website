Start with an image—or a record

Tap Add to choose a photo, take a photograph, import a file or begin without an image. Add the title, year, medium, technique, style, materials, dimensions, edition, condition, location, values, tags and private notes. Attach additional photographs, certificates, documents, audio or video. You can return to edit details later.

Connect your career

Create exhibitions and projects, then select works from your archive. A work can belong to multiple exhibitions and projects without being duplicated. Record venues, dates, curators, statements, milestones and attachments. Studio contains career history, contacts, locations, archive tools and settings.

Free and Pro

Your existing archive stays free, including unlimited artwork records, manual cataloguing, exhibitions, projects, contacts, career history, the widget, backup, restore, CSV export, PDF portfolios, artwork sheets and authenticity certificate templates. Pro adds seven professional document types, a studio cost ledger with proceeds insights, archive completeness checks and 100 AI image suggestions per subscription month. Monthly and annual subscriptions have the same benefits. Current localized prices appear in the app before purchase. There is no free AI access.

The professional toolkit

Open Studio → Professional studio. Document studio creates gallery labels, price lists, packing lists, consignment schedules, condition reports, invoice drafts and insurance schedules from selected artworks. You can select up to 200 works per document. Asking prices and insurance values must use digits with up to two decimal places, without currency symbols or thousands separators. Invoice drafts and consignment schedules use one currency per document; add your recipient, dates, percentages and notes, then verify the generated PDF before sharing.

Costs & proceeds records materials, framing, shipping and other studio expenses, optionally linked to a work. Recorded sales marked Sold are compared with costs separately for each currency. A dated period includes sales with a recorded sale date in that period. No exchange-rate conversion is applied. Expense CSV exports and full archive backups preserve your records. Archive health scans for missing details and duplicate inventory codes without changing your records.

AI suggestions

After subscribing, add an artwork image and choose AI suggestions. Confirm sending the resized image, then review the draft before applying it. An image cannot establish exact materials, dates, dimensions, authenticity, provenance or value. A connection and a verified active purchase are required. If service verification or generation fails, try later; completed suggestions use your allowance. Subscription months are anchored to your original purchase date.

Restore or cancel

Use Studio settings → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Manage or cancel through Apple’s subscription settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. Removing the app does not cancel renewal. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. When Pro ends, your free archive and its existing exports remain available. Saved studio expense records remain readable, editable and exportable; creating new expenses, professional documents, archive scans and AI suggestions requires Pro.

On Android, restore purchases with the Google Play account used to subscribe. Manage renewal in Google Play subscription settings. Android AI suggestion review includes “Report an AI suggestion” for reporting unsafe or inaccurate generated text without leaving the app.

Back up before changing devices

Use Archive tools to export a full archive folder, keep the entire folder and its media together, and save a separate copy in a safe place. Use Restore archive to import the folder on the new device. Android exports a complete archive ZIP and restores missing records from it. iOS archive folders and Android ZIPs are platform-specific; automatic sync or cross-platform restore is not provided. CSV is a spreadsheet export and does not replace a full media backup. Keep independent backups before uninstalling the app.

Widget and large collections

Select artworks for the widget, then add Artwork Inventory from the iOS widget gallery or Android Home Screen widget picker. It requests hourly artwork rotation and refreshes when you open the app. The operating system decides exact refresh times; rotation on every phone unlock is not guaranteed. The archive loads artwork pages and bounded thumbnails to keep large collections responsive. High-resolution originals still require storage space on your device.

Permissions and archive lock

If the camera is unavailable, check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, or the selected camera application and its permissions on Android. Configure a device passcode before enabling archive locking. Selected widget images remain visible outside the lock. For privacy details, read the Privacy Policy.
