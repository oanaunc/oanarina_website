Support for

Ai Shoes Design

Ai Shoes Design — Support

Turn your own sneakers into a creative canvas on iPhone and iPad. For help, email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com and include the Support ID in Settings if your question concerns credits.

Start a Design

  1. Choose a home style, including Peony bloom, Handwritten or Fantasy dragon.
  2. Import a clear shoe photograph from Photos or Files, take a photo, or choose Try a sample shoe.
  3. Optionally add up to three extra angles of the same shoe.
  4. Select paint area, palette and lace color. For handwriting, enter the exact words and select white or gold ink on black.
  5. Choose standard or high detail and one to three alternatives. Read the stated cost, agree to AI processing, and tap Generate.
  6. Completed images are saved as project ideas. Use before-and-after, Save, Share or Export a painting brief.

Free Use with Your Own Key

Open Settings → Add your API key. Create a key at OpenAI, enable API billing and image model access, then validate and save it. Turn on Use my own OpenAI key. ChatGPT subscription fees do not include API usage. OpenAI charges your API account directly; Ai Shoes Design does not add a fee to own-key requests.

Plans and Packs

Monthly: 30 credits/month. Pro: 100 credits/month. Packs: 15 or 50 credits. Local prices are shown before purchase. Standard images cost 1 credit; high detail costs 3; each alternative counts separately. Failed image requests are refunded. Monthly credits reset without rollover; packs do not expire. Credit holders can request up to 10 included poems in 24 hours. Use Restore purchases for an active subscription or unfinished purchase. Apple cannot restore already-finished consumable packs through its purchase history.

If a Generation Is Interrupted

Completed premium images are held for up to 24 hours. Open the project and tap Recover interrupted image if a response was lost. Do not immediately start another paid request. Completed alternatives stay saved if you stop the current job. A job already processing may still finish on the server. Undelivered premium results older than two hours are refunded when your credit balance is next refreshed.

Outline Views and Stencils

Outline views request six sides of the shoe as a clean raster concept sheet. Extra angle photos reduce guesswork, but unseen sides remain AI interpretations. Stencils simplify a selected painted design into a black-on-white tracing concept. Neither is measured CAD, a production pattern or a ready-to-cut vector. Inspect seams, bridges and fit against your actual shoe.

Poems, Learning and Exports

In a saved project, tap Write a shoe poem. The AI uses the design image and brief, then you can copy or share the text. Sneaker School contains twenty illustrated guides, practical paper exercises and reading progress. Export a painting brief creates a local PDF with the source, selected concept, suggested palette, notes and any saved poem.

Common Problems

  • Key rejected: check that the full API key is correct and your account has API access.
  • Billing or rate limit: check OpenAI API funds and account limits, or try again when the service is available.
  • Plans unavailable: check your connection and reload purchase options. App Review uses Apple’s sandbox purchase flow.
  • Photos cannot save: allow add-only photo access in iOS Settings, or Share to Files.
  • Wrong wording or shoe details: simplify the brief and inspect another concept. AI output is never a guarantee of exact geometry or text.

Privacy and Deletion

Projects stay on your device and are excluded from backups; export important work before deleting the app. Delete projects or the credits account in Settings. Account deletion does not cancel a subscription; use Manage subscription first. Read the privacy policy and terms of use.
