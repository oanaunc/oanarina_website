Getting Started

There is no account to create and no password to remember. Where U At uses the iCloud account already signed in on your device, so setup is:

  1. Choose your name, photo and pin colour.
  2. Allow location access.
  3. Tap Add Someone and send the invite link or show the QR code.
  4. When they accept, you appear on each other’s maps.

Sharing is mutual. Nobody can see you until you have both agreed, and either of you can stop at any time.

You Need an iCloud Account

Where U At has no server of its own — it connects people through iCloud. If the app says Sign in to iCloud, open Settings on your device and sign in with your Apple Account. There is no separate Where U At account to create.

Allow Always, or Your Pin Freezes

If you grant only While Using the App, the people you share with will see where you were the last time you opened Where U At — not where you are now. That is how iOS works, and it catches people out.

To fix it: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Where U At › Always. The Privacy tab in the app shows which one you currently have.

Common Problems

They accepted, but I still can’t see them
Both of you need to have granted location permission and to have sharing switched on. Ask them to open the app’s Privacy tab and check that Share My Location is on and that you are not paused for them individually. Pull down on the People list to refresh.

Their pin is hours old
They have almost certainly granted only While Using the App, or their device has been off or without signal. A greyed-out pin means the position is stale.

The invite link does nothing
The person opening it needs Where U At installed and needs to be signed in to iCloud. Each invite link works for one person only — if you want to add somebody else, send a fresh invite.

“Location paused” next to somebody’s name
They have paused sharing, either with everyone or with you specifically. This is deliberate and is their choice to reverse.

I don’t want to be seen right now
Tap your own photo on the map. You can pause for an hour, until tonight, until tomorrow, or until you switch it back on — for everyone, or for one person at a time.

Arrival alerts aren’t arriving
Add the place first on the Places tab, then turn on the alert from that person’s page under Notify Me. Notifications must also be allowed for Where U At in Settings.

Who Can See Me?

The Privacy tab answers this directly: it lists every person who can currently see your location, with a switch beside each one. Turning somebody off there stops your position reaching them without affecting anybody else.

No Location History

Where U At keeps only your most recent position — each new reading replaces the last. There is no record of where you have been, so neither you nor the people you share with can look back at yesterday. This is intentional.

Removing Someone

Open their page and tap Remove Connection. This deletes the copy of your location they were able to read, and you stop seeing them. To reconnect, one of you has to send a new invitation.

Privacy

No account, no servers of ours, no analytics. Your location goes into your own iCloud account and is shared only with people you have accepted. We never see it.
