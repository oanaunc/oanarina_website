Android privacy policy

Your curiosity is private.

Hobby Atlas — Discover Your Next Passion
Effective November 22, 2026 · Android edition

Hobby Atlas does not require an app account. There is no advertising, developer analytics, social network, or developer content server. Your dreams, interests, plans, journal and photos stay in the app’s protected storage on your device. System backups may include them according to your Android settings. There is no automatic in-app cloud sync. Export a backup before uninstalling the app.

The system photo picker gives access only to the photo you choose. A resized copy is stored with your journal. We do not request access to your whole photo library. Notifications are scheduled locally after permission. Calendar export opens your chosen calendar app, where you decide whether to save the event. Rescheduling or deleting a plan here does not change an exported event.

Google Play processes purchases and provides purchase information to verify Plus access. The app checks the Google Play signature and current purchase state. Payment card information is not received by the developer. Purchases are restored separately through your Google Account and are not included in hobby backups.

The optional Plus AI coach uses Gemini Nano on supported Android devices through Google ML Kit and AICore. It is initialized only after you choose to enable it. Questions, hobby context and answers are processed on-device; journal text and photos are never given to the coach. Answers are ephemeral unless you choose to share them. The Google ML Kit SDK sends operational information to Google, including device and app information, identifiers, feature usage, errors and performance metrics. Google may download models and service configurations. The consent screen explains this before the feature is enabled. See Google’s privacy policy and ML Kit terms for details. All curated Plus paths, challenges and insights work without the AI coach.

Exports contain your own notes and photos. You choose where to save or share them. Import replaces the current local memories after confirmation. You can delete saved dreams, plans and journal entries in the app; uninstalling deletes local app data according to Android behavior. System backups and exported copies remain under your control.
