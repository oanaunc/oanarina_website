A little help · Android

Keep your curiosity going.

Discover Your Next Passion · Hobby Atlas for Android phones and tablets

Contact support: oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com

No app login is required. All 504 hobby ideas, search, unlimited dreams, planning, local reminders, journal photos and backups are free. Plus adds seven-day starter paths, a discovery challenge, Passion DNA and an annual recap. Optional on-device AI requires compatible Gemini Nano hardware and a ready system model.

To back up memories, open My World → Prepare a backup and choose a safe location. Restore from a backup validates the file before asking to replace the current memories. The JSON backup format is compatible with the Apple edition. Purchase access is not included in backup files.

To restore Plus, install through the same Google Account used for the purchase and choose Restore Purchases on the Plus screen. Google Play connectivity is required. Apple and Google Play purchases remain separate. Manage and cancel subscriptions in Google Play; uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.

Reminders require notification permission. Android may delay delivery to save battery. Upcoming reminders are refreshed when you open the app and after a device restart; weekly plans cover up to twelve weeks ahead. Calendar export opens your calendar app for confirmation. Exported calendar events are independent of Hobby Atlas plans.

The optional AI coach asks before enabling Google ML Kit. Prompts and answers stay on-device, while Google receives SDK diagnostics and usage metrics. You can use the rest of Plus without enabling the coach. To flag an AI answer, choose Report this idea and review the email draft before sending.

If something goes wrong, include your device model, Android version, app version and what you tried. Send screenshots only if you are comfortable sharing their contents. Never send payment-card details or passwords.
