Privacy Policy for Turbo Racer

Last Updated: 2023

Welcome to Turbo Racer!

At Turbo Racer, our commitment to your privacy is paramount. This document explains how we handle information in relation to your gaming experience in Turbo Racer, a racing game featuring fast cars on the street.

1. Information We Collect

Turbo Racer is designed for your enjoyment without the need to collect any personal information. The game does not require sign-ups or email connections for gameplay.

2. Data Usage

As no personal data is collected, there is no usage or processing of user data.

3. Third-Party Services

Our game is free from third-party services that might collect or use your data.

4. User Consent

By engaging with Turbo Racer, users are considered to have acknowledged and accepted this privacy policy.

5. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

Any changes to our privacy policy will be posted on this page. Users are encouraged to review our privacy policy periodically to stay informed about how we are helping to protect the personal information we collect.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@oanarinaldi.com
