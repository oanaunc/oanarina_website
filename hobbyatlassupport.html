To keep your discoveries safe, open My World → Prepare a backup → Share or save backup. A backup includes personal journal notes and photos; choose its destination carefully.

To restore, choose My World → Restore from a backup. The app validates the file and asks before replacing your memories. Export the current device first if you want to keep both versions. There is no automatic device sync.

For Plus, use Restore Purchases on the Plus screen while signed into the Apple Account used for purchase. Apple handles payment, cancellation and refund requests. Manage subscriptions in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

If a reminder does not appear, check iOS Settings → Notifications → Hobby Atlas. If an event was not added, check the app’s Calendar permission. Exported Apple Calendar events are independent copies; changes in Hobby Atlas do not update them.

There is no app login to reset. Uninstalling may delete local discoveries, so save a backup first. Include the app version, device model and a description of the issue when contacting support; do not send private journal backups unless you choose to share their contents.
